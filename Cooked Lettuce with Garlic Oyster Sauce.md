#Vegetarian #Side #Range 
- 1 head romaine lettuce
- 1 tbsp oil

**Sauce**
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tbsp water

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil
2. Heat the saucier over medium heat, then add the the sauce ingredients in order
3. Bring the sauce to a simmer, then remove from heat and set aside
4. Separate the romaine into individual leaves, wash and drain, leaving the leaves whole
5. Add the oil and lettuce to the boiling water and cook until lightly wilted, 20sec
6. Scoop the lettuce out of the pot, drain excess water, and lay them on a serving plate
7. Pour the sauce over the top and serve immediately
