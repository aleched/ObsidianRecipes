#Dessert #Cookie
- 1½ cup (180g) flour
- 8 tbsp butter
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tbsp heavy cream
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup chocolate chips

1. Heat treat the flour by microwaving in 30sec intervals, stirring between, until 165°
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugar, mixing very well
3. Add the cream and vanilla and mix
4. Add the flour and mix minimally until just combined
5. Add the chocolate chips
