#Dessert #Cake #Oven 
**Cake**
- 6 eggs
- 285g (2¼ cup) flour
- 2¼ tsp baking powder
- ¾ tsp salt
- 100g (½ cup) sugar
- ½ cup whole milk
- 125g (½ cup + 2 tbsp) sugar

**Soak**
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 (14oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- 1 (12oz) can evaporated milk
- 1 tsp vanilla

**Frosting**
- 1½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tbsp powdered sugar

1. Preheat the oven to 350°
2. Butter the center bottom of a 9x13, leaving a ¼ rim and the sides ungreased
3. Separate the eggs into whites and yolks and let warm to room temp
4. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt in a medium bowl
5. Add the egg yolks and the 100g sugar to the mixing bowl and beat until pale and thick, 3min
6. Add the ½ cup of the milk and beat until smooth
7. Add the flour mixture and mix minimally until just combined
8. Transfer the batter to another large bowl and clean the mixing bowl
9. Whip the egg whites until foamy and the yellowish color has disappeared, 1min
10. Slowly add the 125g sugar and continue to beat until shiny stiff peaks form, 2min
11. Stir a big scoop of the egg white mixture into the batter to loosen it
12. Use a spatula to gently fold the remaining whites into the batter without deflating them
13. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top
14. Bake 25-30 min, until the center of the cake is done
#
1. Whisk together the 1 cup milk, condensed milk, evaporated milk and vanilla in a medium bowl
2. Use a skewer to poke holes ½ inch apart all over the cake
3. Cut the edges of the cake from the sides of the pan
4. Pour 1 cup of the milk mixture evenly over the cake and let it soak in for 5-10min, repeat
5. Cover and refrigerate for 2-24 hours

1. Whip the cream with the powdered sugar and frost the cake
