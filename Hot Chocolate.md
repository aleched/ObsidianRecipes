#Drink #Dessert
- ⅓ cup water
- ¼ cup cocoa powder
- 3 cups whole milk
- ¾ cup chocolate chips
- 1 tbsp sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Whipped cream or marshmallows, for topping 

1. Bring the water to a boil in the saucier and whisk in the cocoa
2. Reduce heat to medium and whisk in the milk and bring to a simmer
3. Whisk in the chocolate, sugar, and salt, and reduce heat to low
4. Whisk until the chocolate is melted and very smooth
5. Turn off the heat and whisk in the vanilla
