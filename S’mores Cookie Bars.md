#Dessert #Bar #Oven
- 8 tbsp (113g) butter
- 150g (¾ cup) packed brown sugar
- 1 egg, at room temperature
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 125g (1 cup) flour
- 120g (1 cup) crushed graham crackers (about 8 full sheets)
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 cup (180g) chocolate chips
- 1 heaping cup marshmallows or marshmallow crème

1. Preheat oven to 350°
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the brown sugar, mixing very well
3. Add the egg and vanilla and mix
4. Whisk the flour, graham cracker crumbs, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl
5. Add the dry to the wet and mix until combined
6. Line an 8-inch square baking pan with parchment paper, leaving enough overhang on the sides to easily remove the bars when they have cooled
7. Press ⅔ of the dough into the pan
8. Spread the marshmallow evenly on top
9. Layer chocolate chips evenly on top
10. Mold the remaining cookie dough into flat pieces and layer on top of the chocolate chips
11. Bake for 25-30min or until the top is lightly golden brown
