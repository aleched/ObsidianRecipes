#Dessert #Cake #InstantPot 
- 1½ cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- ⅓ cup oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup water

1. Lightly spray the bundt pan with oil
2. Combine the dry ingredients, mix well
3. Add the oil, vinegar, and vanilla, mix well
4. Add the water and mix until smooth.
5. Pour the batter into the bundt pan and cover with aluminum foil
6. Put the trevet inside the instant pot, add 2 cup water, then place the bundt pan on top
7. Pressure cook for 30min, then quick release
8. Test the cake with a knife, and if still raw pressure cook for 5min more
9. Cool the cake in the pan for 10min, then turn out onto a cooling rack
10. Dust with powdered sugar and serve
