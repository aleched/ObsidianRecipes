#Soup #InstantPot #Vegetarian #Lentil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 1 (14oz) can pumpkin puree
- 1 cup red lentils
- 6 cup broth
- Salt to taste
 
1. Add the onion, garlic, and ginger to the instant pot with the olive oil and saute until softened
2. Add the curry powder and saute until fragrant
3. Add the pumpkin puree, lentils, and broth
4. Pressure cook 5min, natural release 5min, then quick release
5. Add salt to taste and serve
