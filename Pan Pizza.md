#Pizza #Vegetarian 
- 400g (3⅓ cup) flour
- 275g (1 cup) water
- 8g (2 tsp) olive oil
- 10g (2 tsp) salt
- 4g (1 tsp) instant yeast

1. Add ingredients to mixing bowl, beat at medium speed for 30-60 seconds
2. Cover the bowl and let rest at room temp for 8-24hours
#
1. Sprinkle dough with flour and scrape it out onto a floured work surface
2. Flour hands and divide dough into 2 pieces
3. Tuck the dough underneath itself and rotate to shape into a tight ball
4. Pour oil into 2 skillets, add a ball to each, and turn to coat evenly with oil
5. Press the dough into the pans, spreading it to the edges
6. Cover the pans and oven prove for 60min before next step (for a 90min rise total)
#
1. Remove the pans and preheat the oven to 550° for 30min
2. Prepare the toppings while the oven heats
#
1. Once ready, use fingertips to press the dough until it fills every corner of the pan
2. Pop large bubbles and lift edges to let air bubbles underneath escape
3. Add sauce and toppings, spreading to the very edge
4. Sprinkle with salt and drizzle with olive oil
5. Transfer pans to oven and bake until top is golden brown, 12-15min
6. Remove pans from oven, loosen the pizza with a spatula and peek underneath
7. If bottom is not crisp, cook over a medium burner, 1-3min
8. Transfer pizzas to a cutting board, slice and serve
