#Vegetarian #Tofu #Side #Range 
- 8oz silken tofu
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp ginger
- 110g mushrooms
- 140g spinach (about 6 cups)

1. Blend the tofu, soy sauce, mirin, and vinegar until smooth
2. Set the saute pan over medium heat and saute the garlic and ginger in the oil until fragrant, 2-3min
3. Add the mushrooms with salt and cook until they have released their liquid and most has evaporated
4. Add the spinach in batches and cook while stirring until just wilted, 4-5min
5. Add half the tofu mixture and cook until warmed through, 1-2min
6. Add salt to taste
7. Store the rest of the tofu mixture for another batch or to use with other veggies
