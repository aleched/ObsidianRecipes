#Vegetarian #InstantPot #Lentil #Curry 
- 1 cup black gram lentils (Sabut Urad Dal), rinsed and soaked for 4 hours or overnight
- 1/4 cup Red Kidney Beans
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 (14oz) can tomatoes, diced
- 3 cup water or stock, for cooking
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Cilantro, to garnish

**Spices**
- 1 tsp Red Chili powder
- 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp salt

1. Rinse and soak lentils and beans in water for 4 hours or overnight. 
2. Start the pressure cooker in saute mode and let it heat. Add oil and cumin seeds.
3. When the cumin seeds change color, add onions, ginger and garlic. Sauté for about 3 minutes.
4. Add tomatoes and spices. Stir and saute for another 2 minutes. 
5. Drain the water from the soaked lentils and beans. Add them to the instant pot. Add water and mix the ingredients. 
6. (optional) In a steel bowl, add the ingredients for rice. Please a tall trivet and the bowl of rice on top of the trivet. 
7. Press cancel and close the lid with vent in sealing position. Change the instant pot setting to bean/chili, which will set the timer to 30 minutes of high pressure cooking. 
8. When the instant pot beeps, do a natural pressure release. Open the lid. 
9. (optional) Using tongs, carefully take out the bowl of brown rice. Then take out the trivet.
10. Stir the dal well. If needed, add water to get the desired consistency. If you like, add more garam masala to taste. 
11. Stir in the lemon juice and garnish with cilantro. Dal Makhani is ready to be served.
