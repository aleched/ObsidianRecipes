#Bread #Dessert #Oven 
**Dough**
- 530g (4⅓ cup) flour
- 100g (½ cup) granulated sugar
- 175g (¾ cup) lukewarm water (105°)
- 10g (1 tbsp) instant yeast
- 3 eggs
- 150g (10½ tbsp) butter, softened

**Cinnamon paste**
- 213g (1 cup) brown sugar
- 226g (16 tbsp) butter, melted
- 4 tbsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg

**Chocolate paste**
- 180g dark chocolate (70 percent or higher)
- 120g (8 tbsp) butter
- 105g (¾ cup) powdered sugar
- 42g (½ cup + 1 tbsp) cocoa powder
- ¼ tsp nutmeg

1. Add the yeast to the water and let bloom for 8min
2. Add flour and sugar to mixing bowl and whisk together
3. Use dough hook at low speed to add eggs and water/yeast
4. Continue mixing until a dough forms, 5min, scraping down sides as necessary
5. Add butter a spoon at a time and mix until dough is smooth, 10min
6. Shape dough into a ball, put in an oiled bowl, cover and refrigerate 12-24 hours
#
1. Add Cinnamon ingredients to a bowl and mix until smooth
2. Thicken in the freezer to make it spreadable

-OR-

1. Microwave chocolate and butter at medium, stirring every 30sec, until smooth
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix until smooth
#
1. Divide the dough into two and put half back in the fridge
2. Flour a work surface and roll half the dough out into a ½in thick rectangle
3. Spread half the paste onto the dough, leaving a 1in border
4. Roll into a fat log and place in the freezer for 20min, or until firm
5. Repeat with the other half of dough
6. Cut the rolls in half lengthwise with a bread knife, then twist together
7. Butter 2 loaf pans, dust with flour, and place a twist in each
8. Cover and let rest at room temp until doubled, 1-2 hours
#
1. Preheat oven to 375°
2. Bake for 25-30min, or until a tester in the thickest part of the loaf comes out clean
3. Combine ⅓ cup water and ⅓ cup sugar over low heat to make a simple syrup
4. Remove the loaves from the oven, place on a wire rack, and brush with the syrup
5. Let the loaves cool completely before slicing, 1 hour
