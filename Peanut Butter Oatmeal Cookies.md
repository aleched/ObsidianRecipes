#Dessert #Cookie #Oven 
- 1 cup butter
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 2 eggs
- 2¼ cup (270g) flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cup oats
- ½ cup raisins
- ½ cup chocolate chips

1. Preheat the oven to 350°
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugars and peanut butter, mixing very well
3. Add the eggs one at a time and mix
4. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt in a small bowl
5. Add the flour mixture and the oats to the dough and mix minimally until just combined
6. Add the raisins and chocolate chips
7. Spoon onto pans and bake 10min or until done
