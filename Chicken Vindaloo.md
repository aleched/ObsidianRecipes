#Chicken #Curry #InstantPot 
- 2 onions
- 8 cloves garlic
- 4 tsp ginger
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 14oz can tomatoes
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp cayenne
- ½ cup white vinegar
- ¼ cup water
- 2 chicken breasts

1. Dice the onions and saute with the garlic, ginger, and oil until browned, 5-7min
2. Add everything but the chicken to a bowl and use immersion blender to puree
3. Add the chicken and mix well to coat, cover and refrigerate overnight
