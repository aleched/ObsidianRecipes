#InstantPot #Vegetarian #Soup 
- 1½ kg onions
- 6 tbsp butter
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 qt beef or vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp red wine (optional)
- 2 tsp worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 bay leaf

1. Peel and cut the onions into thin wedges
2. Use the instant pot to saute the butter until melted
3. Add the onions and baking soda and cook until the onions are tender, 3-5min
4. Add the garlic and cook for 1min, then turn off the pot
5. Add ¼ cup of the beef stock (or 2 tbsp red wine and 2 tbsp of stock)
6. Pressure cook for 18 minutes, then quick release
7. Saute until the liquid is gone and the onions browned, stirring constantly
8. Add the remaining beef stock, worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, thyme, and bay
9. Bring to a boil, then turn down and simmer for 10min
10. Discard the bay and serve with toasted bread and melted gruyere cheese
