#Noodles #Range #Vegetarian 
- 400g flour
- 200g water
- 8g salt

1. Add the flour, water, and salt to a large bowl and mix by hand until a ball is formed
2. Knead the dough for 5min, then cover and rest for 30min
3. Knead the dough for 5min, then cover and rest for 5min
4. Use a rolling pin to work the dough into a 6x12in rectangle
5. Cut the dough into six pieces, each about 2in wide
6. Brush the pieces with oil and stack them slightly overlapping in an oiled container
7. Cover the container and let rest for at least 1 hour or up to 3 days

1. Let the noodle dough warm outside the fridge and boil a pot of salted water
2. Use a rolling pin to roll the dough piece double the current width and 1in longer
3. Use the back of a knife to imprint the center of the noodle to give it a ripping point
4. Grab the ends and pull the dough gently, stretching until about shoulder length
5. While still pulling, bounce the noodle against the counter until it is about 4ft long
6. Starting in the middle, rip the noodle along the perforation, forming a noodle ring
7. Boil the noodles until they float, ~2min
