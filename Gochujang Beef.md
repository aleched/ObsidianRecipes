#Beef #Range 
- 225g ground beef
- 100g peas
- 50g gochujang
- salt to taste
- drizzle of sesame oil

1. Sear the ground beef in a pan over medium heat with a sprinkle of salt
2. When the beef is almost cooked through, add the gochujang and peas
3. Mix until well combined, and add a splash of water if the mixture is too tight
4. Stir and reduce until the sauce glazes the meat
5. Salt to taste and serve over rice with a drizzle of sesame oil
