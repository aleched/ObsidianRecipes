#Bread #Range
- 175g maseca
- 240g water

**Stuffing Suggestions**
- Refried beans & cheese
- Shredded or diced meat (like tinga poblana or carnitas)
- Fresh fillings: avocado, cabbage slaw, plantains, cilantro & onion, and salsa​

1. Preheat the oven to 400°
2. Mix the masa harina and water
3. Roll a golf-ball sized dough sample and press it flat between your hands. The edges of the flattened dough should be smooth, not cracking. Add a bit more water until the dough passes the smush test. The masa should be moist but not sticky.
#
1. For each gordita, start with a 2” (5 cm) diameter ball of dough. Roll it into a uniform sphere and then flatten it with your palms into an even puck. It should be about 1/4-1/2” (10 mm) in thickness and resemble a small pancake.
2. In a cast iron pan over medium-high heat, cook all the gorditas for 3 minutes per side or until lightly charred on the exterior.
3. To finish cooking, slide the gorditas into the oven (on the same pan is fine) for an additional 4 minutes.
4. Stuff & serve: While the gorditas are still warm, use the tip of a knife to cut around half the perimeter and open it up into a stuffable pocket, gently using your fingers to widen the interior, which should be cooked but still soft/chewy in the center.
5. Stuff with your preferred ingredients and serve warm.
