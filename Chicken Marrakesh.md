#Chili #InstantPot #Cassarole 
- 3 carrots
- 2 sweet potatoes
- 1 onion
- 2 chicken breasts
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp parsley
- 1 15oz can garbanzo beans
- 1 14 can tomatoes

1. Cut the chicken into chunks and lightly season with salt and pepper
2. Cut the onions into wedges, the carrots rangiri, and the potatoes into chunks
3. Drain and rinse the garbanzo beans
4. Add everything to the pot and mix well
5. Pressure cook for 10min, naturally release for 5min, then quick release
