#Range #Vegetarian 
- 228g (2 cups) shredded Gruyere cheese
- 171g (1½ cups) shredded Swiss cheese
- 19g (2 tbsp) cornstarch
- 1 cup broth
- ¼ tsp lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or pressed
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- ¼ tsp paprika
- ¼ tsp onion powder 
- ¼ tsp MSG
- ¼ tsp pepper
- salt to taste

1. Add the cheeses and cornstarch to a ziplock bag and shake until evenly coated
2. Add the broth, lemon juice, and garlic to the fondue pot and bring to a simmer
3. Reduce the heat to medium-low and add the cheese a bit at a time while gently constantly stirring, scraping the bottom of the pot so it doesn’t burn
5. Once the cheese is melted and begins to just barely simmer, add the remaining spices and serve immediately
6. Dippers: steamed broccoli or cauliflower, carrots, apples, chicken sausages, bread, lightly sauteed mushrooms, tender potatoes, roasted tofu
