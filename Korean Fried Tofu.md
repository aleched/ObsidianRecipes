#Tofu #Fry #Vegetarian 
- 1 package firm tofu
- ¼ cup cornstarch

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp ketchup
- 4 tbsp rice syrup
- 3 tbsp gochujang

1. Heat oil in the dutch oven to 330-340°
2. Cut the tofu into bite sized pieces and pat dry with paper towels
3. Gently toss the tofu with the cornstarch, then transfer to a plate individually spaced
4. Drop the tofu into the oil one by one and fry until all sides are light brown, 5min
5. Drain on a paper towel on a wire rack set over a baking sheet
6. Reheat the oil and repeat with the additional batches of tofu if necessary
7. Reheat the oil and fry a second time until all sides are golden brown, 5min
8. Drain and repeat with the additional batches
9. Add the sauce ingredients to a skillet and stir over low heat until bubbling
10. Add the tofu to the sauce and stir until well coated, serve with rice
