#Chicken #Cassarole #InstantPot 
- 900g chicken thighs or breasts
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 1 14.5oz can diced tomatoes
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 7.5oz can chipotle chili peppers in adobo sauce
- 3 tbsp cornstarch
- 3 tbsp water, cold

1. Dice the onion and add all ingredients up to the chipotle to the instant pot
2. Pressure cook for 15min (+3min if frozen)
3. Naturally release for 10min, then quick release
4. Remove the chicken and set aside
5. Use immersion blender to puree the sauce
6. Make a slurry with the cornstarch and water and add to the pot
7. Saute the sauce for a couple minutes until thickened
8. Shred the chicken and stir into the sauce
9. Add in lime juice to taste
