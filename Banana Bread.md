#Dessert #Bread #Oven 
- ½ cup butter
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- 3-5 overripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt

1. Preheat oven to 350°
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugar, mixing very well
3. Add the bananas and eggs and mix
4. Whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl
5. Stir banana mixture into flour mixture, mixing just to moisten
6. Pour into the loaf pan and bake 60-70min, until center is not raw
7. Rest in the pan 10min, then turn out onto a wire rack
