#Chicken #Cassarole #Oven #Range 
**Dumplings**
- 180g (1½ cup) flour
- 9g (2¼ tsp) baking powder
- 3g (½ tsp) salt
- 1 tbsp thyme
- 57g (½ cup) grated cheddar
- 270g (1 cup + 3 tbsp) heavy cream

**Stew**
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 3 tbsp flour
- 2 cup chicken broth
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 3 cup shredded chicken
- 3 cup frozen peas and carrots
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper

1. Preheat oven to 500°
2. Mix all dumpling ingredients except the cream together in a bowl
3. Add the cream and mix until a shaggy dough forms, refrigerate until ready
4. Heat saute pan over medium-high heat, add oil
5. Saute onions until browned, 3 to 4 minutes
6. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more
7. Add the flour and stir until dissolved
8. Stir in the chicken broth, milk, and cream and bring to a boil
9. Reduce to a simmer, stirring constantly until thickened, 5 to 8 minutes
10. Remove heat and stir in the chicken, peas and carrots, salt, and pepper
11. Shape the dumpling dough into 6 ½ inch thick patties and place on top of the stew
12. Bake uncovered until the dumplings are browned, 20-30 minutes
