#Vegetarian #Side #Oven 
- 450-900g button or cremini mushrooms
- 1 tbsp olive oil per 225g mushrooms

1. Preheat oven to 375°
2. Trim, quarter, and wash the mushrooms
3. Toss the mushrooms in the olive oil with salt and pepper
4. Spread in an even layer on a foil-lined baking sheet
5. Cook 15min, then drain the released liquid into a bowl and reserve for another use
6. Cook until browned, 30min
