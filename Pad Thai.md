#Noodles #Range #Vegetarian #Chicken 
- 8 oz flat rice noodles
- 1 chicken breast or tofu block
- 1 onion
- 1 red bell pepper
- 3 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup peanuts
- Lime juice

Sauce

- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp lime juice

1. Soak the noodles until tender, up to an hour in advance
2. Cut the chicken or tofu, onion, and pepper into thin strips
3. Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small bowl
5. Heat a large pan over medium-high heat, add 2 tbsp oil
6. Cook the chicken or tofu, onion, pepper, and garlic until done, 3-5min
7. Make space in the pan, add 1 tbsp more oil and scramble the eggs
8. Add the noodles, sauce, and peanuts, mix well
9. Serve with a splash of lime juice garnished with extra peanuts
