#Noodles #Range #Vegetarian #Chicken 
- 1 chicken breast or tofu block
- 1 onion
- 1 red bell pepper
- 8 oz flat rice noodles
- 3 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup peanuts
- Lime juice

Sauce

- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp lime juice

1. Cut the chicken or tofu, onion, and pepper into thin strips
2. Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small bowl
3. Boil noodles until tender, rinse under cold water
4. Heat a large pan over medium-high heat, add 2 tbsp oil
5. Cook the chicken or tofu, onion, pepper, and garlic until done, 3-5min
6. Make space in the pan, add 1 tbsp more oil and scramble the eggs
7. Add the noodles, sauce, and peanuts, mix well
8. Serve with a splash of lime juice garnished with extra peanuts
