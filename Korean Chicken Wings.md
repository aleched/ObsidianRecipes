#Chicken #Fry 
- 900g chicken wings
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ cup cornstarch
- ¼ cup flour
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 2 eggs

Sauce

- 2 tsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- ⅓ cup ketchup
- ⅓ cup rice syrup
- ¼ cup gochujang
- 2 tsp vinegar

1. Heat oil in the dutch oven to 330-340°
2. Mix the wings and batter together in a large bowl by hand
3. Drop the wings into the oil one by one and fry until all sides are light brown, 12min
4. Drain on a paper towel on a wire rack set over a baking sheet
5. Reheat the oil and repeat with the additional batches of wings if necessary
6. Reheat the oil and fry a second time until all sides are golden brown, 12min
7. Drain and repeat with the additional batches
8. Sauté the garlic with the oil in a skillet until light brown, 30sec
9. Add the rest of the sauce ingredients, stir over low heat until bubbling
10. Add the wings to the sauce and stir until well coated, serve with kimchi
