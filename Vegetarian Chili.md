#Vegetarian #Chili #Range 
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 onion
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tbsp oregano
- 1 tbsp salt
- 2 stalks celery
- 2 bell peppers
- 2 jalapeno peppers
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 4oz cans chopped green chile peppers
- 2 12oz packages vegetarian burger crumbles
- 3 28oz cans whole peeled tomatoes
- ¼ cup chili powder
- 1 tbsp  black pepper
- 1 15oz can kidney beans
- 1 15oz can garbanzo beans
- 1 15oz can black beans
- 2 cups frozen corn

1. Dice the onion, celery, peppers, and garlic
2. Drain and rince the beans, drain the canned green chile peppers, crush the canned tomatoes
3. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat
4. Add the onion, bay leaves, cumin, oregano, and salt and cook until softened, 5min
5. Add the celery, bell peppers, jalapeno peppers, garlic, and green chile peppers and cook 5min
6. Add the vegetarian burger crumbles and reduce heat to low, cover and simmer 5min
7. Add the rest of the ingredients, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 45min
