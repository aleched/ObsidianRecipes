#Chicken #Curry #InstantPot 
- 900g meat (chicken or lamb)
- 2 onions
- ½ cup greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 6 cloves garlic
- 4 tbsp basil
- 4 tbsp parsley
- 4 tsp ginger
- 4 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ¼ cup water
- ½ cup greek yogurt

1. Cut the meat into chunks and dice the onion
2. Add everything up to the water excluding meat to a bag, mix well
3. Add the meat and mix well to coat, cover and refrigerate overnight
#
1. Empty the bag into the instant pot, add the water, mix well
2. Pressure cook 10min for chicken or 15min for lamb, then naturally release
3. Add the other ½ cup greek yogurt, mix well
