#Sauce #Vegetarian 
- 1 (14oz) can tomatoes
- ¼ onion
- 2-3 garlic cloves
- 2-3 chipotles in adobo & their liquid
- Sprinkle of cumin
- Sprinkle of oregano
- Salt to taste

1. Add everything to a blender and process until smooth
2. Set a pan over medium-high heat, add a little oil, then pour in the sauce
3. Simmer 2-3min or until the sauce has darkened a shade
4. Add cooked protein (shredded chicken, pork, beef, tofu)
5. Lower the heat and gently simmer 10-15min to reduce and meld the flavors
