#Oven #Beef 
- 3 lbs bone-in beef short ribs
- 1 large onion
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 3 cup liquid (beer, wine, or low-sodium broth)
- 2 tsp rosemary

1. Preheat oven to 325°
2. Season ribs generously with salt and pepper
3. Cut onion into wedges
4. Heat sauté pan over medium-high heat, add oil
5. Add ribs in a single layer and sear each side for several minutes until browned
6. Turn down the heat and add the onion and garlic, cook until tender, 3-5min
7. Add the liquid and rosemary, bring to a simmer
8. Cover and place in the oven, cook until very tender, 2-3 hours
9. Rest covered for 20min
