#Fish #Range 
- 700g tilapia fillets
- 4 tbsp miso
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp ginger

1. Whisk the sauce together in a small bowl
2. Marinate the fish in the sauce for at least 30min
3. Sear the fish over medium high heat until white and fully cooked, 4-5min
4. Thicken the remainder of the sauce in the skillet or microwave and drizzle over the fillets
