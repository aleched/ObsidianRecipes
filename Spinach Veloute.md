#Vegetarian #Soup 
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 leek
- ½ onion
- Salt and pepper
- 4 cloves garlic
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1 qt chicken or vegetable stock
- 2 packed quarts of spinach or romaine or broccoli
- 1 splash lemon juice

1. Melt the butter with a splash of olive oil in the saute pan over medium heat
2. Remove the roots and leaves of the leek and cut it in half lengthwise, rinse it, cut in half again, then roughly chop
3. Roughly dice the onion and add it to the butter with the leek and some salt and pepper
4. Sweat out the onion and leek until soft but not browned, 3min
5. Add the garlic and continue cooking until fragrant, 30sec
6. Add the flour and cook until a paste forms, 30sec
7. Add the stock and increase heat to high, cook while stirring until the stock is simmering and thickened
8. Add the spinach or romain or broccoli and any herbs like parsley and cook until bright green, 30sec
9. Transfer to a blender and remove the center plug to allow steam to escape, use a dish cloth instead
10. Blend at low speed and gradually increase to high until completely smooth
11. Season with more salt and pepper to taste and add a splash of lemon juice
