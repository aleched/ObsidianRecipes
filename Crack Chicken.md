#InstantPot #Chicken #Cassarole 
- 900g chicken thighs or breast
- 1 8oz package cream cheese
- 3 tbsp ranch seasoning
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- ½ cup water
- 8 strips bacon
- 1 cup cheddar cheese

1. Add everything up to the bacon to the pot
2. Pressure cook for 15min (+3min if frozen)
3. Naturally release for 10min, then quick release
4. While the chicken is cooking, cook the bacon
5. Remove the chicken and shred
6. Add the chicken, bacon, and cheddar cheese and mix
