#Vegetarian #Side #Range 
- 1 (15oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tsp oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp salt, or to taste
- black pepper to taste

1. Heat a skillet over medium heat. Add the oil and chickpeas.
2. Fry the chickpeas for 2-3 minutes, or until they get a bit of color.
3. Add the spices, salt, and pepper and stir to coat. Cook another 2-3 minutes to toast the spices and brown the chickpeas slightly.
