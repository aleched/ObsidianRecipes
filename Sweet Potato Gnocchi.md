#Noodles #Range #Vegetarian 
- 450g sweet potatoes
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- 1 egg
- 2 cup flour

1. Wrap the sweet potatoes in wet paper towels and microwave until done
2. Let the potatoes cool, then peel them
3. Add everything but the flour to the mixing bowl and beat until smooth
4. Add the flour a little at a time until a soft dough forms

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil
2. Roll the dough out into several long ropes on a floured surface, then cut them into pieces
3. Drop the pieces into the boiling water to cook until they float
4. Remove the floating pieces with the spider and keep them warm in a serving dish
