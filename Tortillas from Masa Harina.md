#Bread #Range 
- 170g masa harina
- 220g water, lukewarm
- 1 pinch of salt

1. Heat the griddle over medium
2. Mix the masa, water, and salt in a large bowl until cohesive
3. Add enough masa that the dough isn't sticky and enough water that it isn't crumbly, it should have the consistancy of play-doh
4. Divide the dough into ~55g balls
5. Flatten a ball between the sheets of a ziplock bag with the pie tin
6. Cook 30sec on the first side, until dry but not browned
7. Flip and cook 90sec on the second side, push down on the tortillas towards the end
8. Flip and cook 15sec on the first side again, push down again, tortilla should puff
9. Keep moist under a kitchen towel until ready to serve
