#Bread #Range 
- 500g flour
- 10g salt
- 30g oil
- 300g water

1. Add the flour, salt, and oil to the mixing bowl and mix together
2. Add the water and mix until no dry flour is left, cover and rest 15min
#
1. Knead the dough until it passes the gluten window test, 5min
2. Divide the dough into 8-10 even pieces
3. Spread them out on a baking sheet, cover and rest 15min
#
1. Flour the work surface and rolling pin
2. Roll a piece of dough into a circle as thin as possible, until slightly translucent
3. Spread ghee all over the dough then sprinkle flour over the ghee
4. Accordion fold the dough into multiple layers, then roll it into a tight circle
5. Tuck the end of the dough into the center of the circle, then press it into a puck
6. Repeat with the remaining pieces of dough, then cover and let rest 15min
#
1. Roll each puck of coiled dough into a circle 8 inches in diameter and cook
#
1. Set a pan over medium heat and add ghee
2. Cook the paratha until both sides are golden brown

OR

1. Set a pan over medium-low heat
2. Dry cook the paratha 2-3min per side until no dry spots remain, cool on a wire rack
3. Place the paratha in a ziplock bag separated by parchment paper and freeze
4. Cook from frozen as normal
