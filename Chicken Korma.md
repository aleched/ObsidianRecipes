#Curry #Chicken #InstantPot 
- 2 onions
- 1 14oz can tomatoes
- 1 hot pepper (jalapeno, serrano, etc)
- 60g cashews or almonds
- 8 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp ginger
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 2 chicken breasts
- 1 cup coconut milk or heavy cream
- 2 tsp garam masala

1. Add everything up to the chicken to the instant pot and use immersion blender to puree
2. Add the chicken and push down into the sauce
3. Pressure cook for 10min (+2min if frozen), then naturally release
4. Remove the chicken, cut into chunks, and add back
5. Add coconut milk or heavy cream and the additional garam masala, mix well
