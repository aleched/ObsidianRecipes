#Chicken #Oven 
- 1 whole 4 lb chicken
- 1+ qt water
- ⅓ cup kosher salt
- 2 tsp peanut oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt

1. Use the water and salt to make a brine
2. Empty the chicken’s cavity and place it in the brine breast side down
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight
#
1. Put the dutch oven into the cold oven and set temp to 550°, let warm for 30min
2. Drain and dry the chicken with paper towels, let it warm to room temp
3. Rub the chicken with the oil and sprinkle with salt
4. Remove hot pot from oven, lightly coat inside with oil
5. Carefully drop the chicken in breast side up
6. Cover the pot, put back into the oven
7. Cook for 15min, baste with juices and cook for 15min more
8. Remove the dutch oven and rest with lid on for 10min
9. Remove the lid and rest for 5min more
10. Thermometer should read at least 150° at thickest, carryover heat will finish
11. Gently lift the chicken out, tilting it to drain any hot juices into the pot
12. Pour the juices from the pot into a bowl to accompany the meat
