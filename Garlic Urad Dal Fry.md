#Vegetarian #Curry #Lentil #InstantPot #Range 
- 2 cups (414g) urad dal
- 5 cups (1135g) stock or water
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or coconut oil
- 2 tsp whole cumin seed
- 5-6 cloves garlic, peeled and roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp minced ginger
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 4 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt to taste

1. Add the urad dal, stock, coriander powder, turmeric, and salt to the instant pot
2. Pressure cook for 25min, naturally release for 15min, then quick release
3. While the pressure is releasing, heat the oil over medium heat in a large skillet
4. Add the cumin seed and let fry until fragrant, 30sec
5. Add the garlic and ginger and fry until the garlic is starting to turn golden in places, 1-2min
6. Stir in the garam masala and lemon juice and add the cooked urad dal
7. Bring to a simmer and add salt to taste, and adjust any other spices to taste
8. Simmer 5-10min to meld the flavors, add water as needed to thin to your desired consistency
9. Serve garnished with pickled onions
