#Bread #Range
- ½ cup lukewarm water (105°)
- 2 tsp instant yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 egg
- ¼ cup yogurt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp salt
- 2-3 cup flour

1. Mix water, yeast, and sugar and let sit for 5min or until bubbly
2. Add egg, yogurt, olive oil, salt, and 2 cups of flour and mix until smooth
3. Add enough flour to make a soft dough and knead a few times until smooth
4. Move dough to a greased bowl, cover and oven prove low 60min until doubled
#
1. Preheat a skillet to medium heat
2. Cut the dough into 8 pieces and roll each out into a 6" circle on a floured surface
3. Lightly oil the skillet and cook 2-3min, until bubbly and brown
4. Flip over and cook for another 2-3min
5. Brush the top of each naan with ghee and garlic
