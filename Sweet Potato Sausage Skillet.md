#Range 
- 1 lb sweet potato
- ½ lb sausage (removed from casing)
- ½ onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 15oz can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup rice, uncooked
- 1 cup salsa
- 1¾ cup chicken broth
- 1 cup cheese

1. Cut the sweet potato into small ½in chunks, and the onion into wedges
2. Heat saute pan over medium heat, add oil
3. Saute the sweet potatoes until they have some color, 5min
4. Add the onion and sausage and cook until browned
5. Add the garlic, fish sauce, chili powder, beans, rice, salsa and mix well
6. Add the chicken broth, cover, and bring to a boil
7. Lower to a simmer and cook covered for 30min
8. Once the rice has cooked, top with the cheese and serve
