- 4 cups (175g) cubes of crusty bread
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 pound ground pork sausage, casings removed
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary or Italian seasoning, optional
- 3/4 cup (100g) chopped yellow onion (1/2 of a large onion)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cups (275g) chopped bell peppers
- 1 cup (100g) sliced mushrooms
- 1 cup roughly chopped fresh spinach
- 12 large eggs
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2/3 cup (160ml) milk
- 1 cup (100g) shredded cheddar cheese

1. Grease a 9×13 dish.
2. Arrange the bread cubes in an even layer in bottom of dish.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add sausage and rosemary/Italian seasoning, if using, and break up the sausage into bite-size pieces with a wooden spoon or rubber spatula as it begins to cook.
4. Add the onion, garlic, peppers, mushrooms, and spinach and cook until everything has slightly softened and sausage is mostly cooked through, about 5–6 minutes.
5. Remove sausage and vegetable mixture from heat and spread in an even layer on top of bread.
6. Whisk the eggs, salt, pepper, milk, and cheese together. Evenly pour over sausage/vegetable mixture. Add another sprinkle of salt and pepper on top.
7. Cover casserole with plastic wrap or aluminum foil and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes and up to 24 hours.
#
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and remove the casserole from the fridge.
2. Bake uncovered 40-45min; until the top is golden, edges are crisp, and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
3. Cool for 10 minutes, then slice and serve.
