#Side #InstantPot 
- 1 lb dry navy beans
- 1 lb bacon
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup bourbon
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ¾ cup BBQ sauce
- ¼ cup ketchup
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp mustard
- 2 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp gochujang

1. Add beans to the instant pot inner pot and cover with water, let soak overnight
#
1. Drain the beans and cover them again with fresh water
2. Pressure cook for 20min, naturally release for 10min, then quick release
3. Drain the beans and set aside in a bowl
4. Dice the bacon, onion, and garlic
5. Saute the bacon in the instant pot until crispy, 5min
6. Add the onion and saute until softened and browned, 10min
7. Add the garlic and saute until fragrant, 30sec
8. Add the beans back in along with the rest of the ingredients, mix well
9. Slow cook low for 10-12 hours, stirring occasionally
10. Let sit on keep warm for 1-2 hours to allow the sauce to thicken
