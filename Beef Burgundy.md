#Beef #Range #Cassarole #InstantPot 
- 2 lbs stewing beef
- 1 onion
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 bottle (750 ml) red wine 1 bottle
- 1 cup beef stock (low sodium)
- 2 tsp thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp flour
- 1 tbsp water

1. Cube the beef and season generously with salt and pepper
2. Dice the onion, halve the mushrooms
3. Use the instant pot to saute the beef in the oil until browned on all sides, then remove and set aside
4. Saute the butter until melted
5. Add the onion and mushrooms, saute until tender, 3-5min
6. Add the garlic and saute 1min
7. Add the tomato paste and saute 1min
8. Add the beef, wine, stock, thyme, and bay, mix well
9. Pressure cook for 25min, naturally release for 10min, then quick release
10. Whisk together flour and water in a small bowl, then add to the pot
11. Saute until thickened, stirring constantly
12. Discard the bay and serve garnished with parsley
