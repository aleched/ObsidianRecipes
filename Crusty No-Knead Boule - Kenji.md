#Bread #Oven 
**Poolish**
- 280g (2⅓ cup) flour
- 280g (1 cup) water, lukewarm
- 1g (¼ tsp) yeast
- 1g (¼ tsp) salt

**Dough**
- 120g (1 cup) flour
- 8g salt (2½ tsp) salt
- 1g (¼ tsp) yeast
- Several drops of vinegar or lemon juice

1. Mix the poolish in a bowl until no dry flour is left, cover and rest for 8-12 hours
#
1. Add the dry dough ingredients to a large bowl and hand mix until homogeneous
2. Add the vinegar to the poolish, then add the poolish to the dry ingredients
3. Hand mix until the dough is a sticky, shaggy, single mass, 30-45sec
4. Invert a medium bowl over the dough in the large bowl and put some weight on top
5. Let the dough rest for 15min
6. With wet hands, reach under a side of the dough then stretch and fold it into the center, rotate and repeat for the other 3 sides
7. Repeat the 15min rest followed by the stretch and fold 3-4 more times
8. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or for up to about 7 days (more time = more tang)
#
1. Remove the dough from the fridge and let it come up to room temp, 1-8hours
#
1. Dry out the medium bowl, then line it with a handkerchief thoroughly dusted by flour
2. With floured hands, quickly and gently do one last stretch and fold
3. Scrape the dough out onto a floured work surface and pinch the folds into a seam
4. Flip the dough seam-side down and with extra flour as necessary, tuck it together underneath using the sides of your hands palms up, until the top is smooth and taut
5. Pinch the seam then place it seam-side down into the bowl and dust with flour
6. Cover the bowl with a baking sheet and prove 90min before starting the next step
#
1. Preheat the oven to 500° for 30min
2. Invert the bowl and baking sheet so the dough is on the sheet
3. Remove the bowl and carefully remove the handkerchief
4. Splash some water inside the large bowl, then invert it over the dough
5. Transfer to the oven, reduce the temp to 450°, and bake for 20min
6. Remove the bowl and bake 15-20min more until loaf is browned
7. Remove the loaf from the oven and let it cool completely on a rack before slicing
