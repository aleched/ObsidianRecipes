#InstantPot #Vegetarian #Cassarole #Lentil 
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ cup brown rice
- ½ cup lentils
- 3 cup water or stock
- 1 tsp salt

1. Saute the rice in the olive oil using the instant pot until lightly toasted, 2min
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix well
3. Pressure cook for 15min, naturally release for 10min, then quick release
