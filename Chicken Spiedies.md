#Chicken #Range 
- 900g chicken (breast or thighs)
- 9g salt

**Marinade**
- 50g mayo
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp black pepper

**Sauce**
- 50g olive oil
- 25g apple cider vinegar
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp parsley
- 1 tsp oregano
- ¼ tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp MSG
- ¼ tsp salt

1. Weigh out the frozen chicken, then thaw it in a bowl of warm water
#
1. Cut the chicken into bite-size cubes, then pat it dry with paper towels
2. Add the chicken back into the bowl with the salt and mix
3. Let dry brine in the fridge uncovered for at least 1 hour or overnight
#
1. Prepare the marinade and sauce, refrigerate until ready to use
2. Add the marinade to the bowl of chicken and mix until each piece is coated
3. Thread the chicken onto skewers (optional)
4. Set a grill or griddle over high heat until smoking
5. Add the chicken and cook until there is deep browning on one side before flipping
6. Cook until the chicken is charred and cooked through, 8min
7. Drizzle with the sauce and serve on a hoagie roll with pickled onions, lettuce, tomato, feta, etc
