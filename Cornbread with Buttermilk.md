#Bread #Oven 
- 1 ¼ cup cornmeal
- 1 ¼ cup flour
- ¼ cup sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1¾ cup buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 4 tbsp butter, cut into 4 pieces

1. Preheat the oven to 425°
2. Add the dry ingredients to a bowl and mix well
3. Add the buttermilk and egg and slowly mix until it just comes together
4. Add the butter to a cast iron skillet and cook in the oven 3-5min, until melted
5. Remove the pan from the oven and swirl the butter in the pan to evenly coat
6. Add the batter to the pan and smooth the top with a spatula
7. Bake for 20-25min, until golden-brown and cooked through
8. Remove from the oven and cool 5 minutes before cutting and serving
