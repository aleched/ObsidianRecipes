#Dessert #Cake #Oven 
- 8 tbsp butter
- ¾ cup sugar
- 150g (1¼ cup) flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 eggs
- Plums, enough to fill the pan

1. Preheat oven to 350°
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugar, mixing very well
3. Add the flour, baking powder, and eggs and mix well
4. Spread the batter in an 8inch pan
5. Quarter the plums and tightly pack them skin side up on top of the batter
6. Sprinkle with sugar and lemon juice, and optionally cinnamon or nugmeg
7. Bake 40-60min, until plums are soft
