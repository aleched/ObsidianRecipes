#Oven #Chicken
- 1-4 lbs chicken wings, cut into drumettes and flats
- 1 tsp baking powder (per lb of wings)
- 1 tsp corn starch (per lb of wings)
- 1 tsp salt (per lb of wings)
- ½ tsp garlic powder (per lb of wings)
- ½ tsp onion powder (per lb of wings)

1. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil and set a wire rack inside
2. Cut the wings at the joint to make drumettes and flats, trim and discard the tips
3. Dry the wings with paper towels, then toss with baking powder and salt
4. Evenly space the wings on the prepared rack
5. Refrigerate the wings uncovered overnight
#
1. Preheat oven to convection 450°
2. Cook for 20min, then flip wings and cook 20min until crisp
