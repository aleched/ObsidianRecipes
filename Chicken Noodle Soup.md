#InstantPot #Chicken #Noodles #Soup 
- 1 onion
- 2 carrots
- 3 stalks celery
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp parsley
- 1 tsp oregano
- 4 cup chicken broth
- 4 cup water
- 2 chicken breasts
- 2 cup egg noodles

1. Cut the onions into wedges and chop the carrots and celery
2. Use the instant pot to saute the vegetables in the ghee until the onion is tender, 3-5min
3. Add the seasonings and mix well
4. Add the chicken broth, water, and chicken breasts
5. Pressure cook on the soup setting for 7min (+2min if frozen), then naturally release
6. Remove the chicken breasts and shred
7. Add the noodles and saute for 6 minutes or until they are cooked
8. Add the shredded chicken back
