#Bread #Oven 
**Poolish**
- 350g (3 cup) flour
- 350g (1½ cup) milk
- 1g (¼ tsp) yeast
- 1g (¼ tsp) salt

**Dough**
- 42g (3 tbsp) butter, melted
- 2 eggs
- 350g (3 cup) flour
- 25g (2 tbsp) sugar
- 15g (1 tbsp) salt
- 4g (1½ tsp) yeast

1. Mix the poolish in a bowl until no dry flour is left, cover and rest for 8-12 hours
#
1. Add the butter and eggs to a mixing bowl and beat until combined
2. Add the flour, sugar, salt, and yeast to a medium bowl and whisk together
3. Add the poolish and flour to the mixing bowl and beat until smooth, 2min
4. Use dough hook to knead until stiff and slightly tacky, 10min (add flour if necessary)
5. Transfer to a buttered bowl, flip greased side up, cover and let rise for 1½-2 hours
#
1. When dough has doubled, turn it out onto a floured surface and knead for 3min
2. Return to greased bowl, cover and let rise again for 30min
#
1. Divide dough in half, shape each half into a log, and place them into loaf pans
2. Brush tops of loaves with ghee
3. Cover and let rise until dough is just above the tops of pans, 45-60min
#
1. Bake bread at 400° for 10min
2. Reduce heat to 350° and bake an additional 20min, until golden brown
3. Remove loaves from pans and let them cool on wire racks before slicing
