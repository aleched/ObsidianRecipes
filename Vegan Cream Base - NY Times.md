#Dessert #IceCream 
- 2 cup cashew or hemp milk
- 1 14oz can coconut cream or whole coconut milk
- ½ cup light corn syrup or ⅓ cup agave syrup
- ½ cup  sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp vodka (optional)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

1. Add everything up to the vodka to the saucier
2. Simmer until the sugar dissolves, 5min
3. Transfer to a container and chill at least 3 hours
4. Stir in the vodka and vanilla
