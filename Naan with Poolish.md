#Bread #Range
**Poolish**
- 150g (1¼ cup) flour
- 150g (⅔ cup) lukewarm water
- 2g (½ tsp) instant yeast

**Dough**
- 300g poolish
- 475g (4 cup) flour
- 50g (¼ cup) heavy cream
- 25g (⅛ cup) milk or water
- 2g (½ tsp) instant yeast
- 125g yogurt
- 50g sugar
- 15g salt
- 60g water (add last)

1. Mix the poolish in a bowl until no dry flour is left, cover and rest for 12 hours
#
1. Add all ingredients but water to the mixing bowl, combine with the dough hook
2. Mix at low speed for 2min to incorporate ingredients
3. Mix at medium speed for 2min, and slowly add half the water
4. Mix at high speed for 2min and finish adding the water, mix until smooth
5. Shape the dough into a ball, transfer to a bowl, cover and rest for 4 hours
#
1. Divide the dough into roughly 140g pieces and roll them into tight balls
2. Cover with a damp paper towel or kitchen towel and rest for 90min
#
1. Add ½ cup of ghee to a small bowl with minced garlic
2. Roll out the dough balls on a floured surface to about 6-7in
3. Heat a cast iron skillet on high until smoking, then lower to medium and brush with the garlic ghee
4. Place naan on the hot pan and press down with a spatula
5. Brush the top side with the garlic ghee
6. Cook 1min, until dark spots form
7. Flip, but don’t press down with a spatula
8. Cook 1min until dark spots form
9. Remove from the pan, brush with garlic ghee and sprinkle with salt
