#Chicken #Noodles #InstantPot #Cassarole 
- 6 chicken thighs
- 1 lb penne pasta
- ¾ cup Italian salad dressing
- 1 qt chicken broth
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 (8oz) block cream cheese
- 2 cup spinach
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes
- ½ cup sundried tomatoes
- ½ cup pecorino cheese
- 2 tbsp basil
- 2 tbsp oregano
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp water

1. Cut chicken thighs into small chunks and add to the instant pot in an even layer
2. Add the pasta, dressing, broth, pepper, and cream cheese, don’t mix
3. Pressure cook for 3min, natural release for 5min, then quick release
4. Add the rest of the ingredients except the flour
5. Make a slurry with the flour and water and add to the pot, mix well
