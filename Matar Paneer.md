#Vegetarian #Curry #InstantPot 
- ⅓ block (300g) paneer
- 2 onions
- 2 14oz can tomatoes
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp ginger
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tbsp salt
- ¼ cup water
- 3 cup frozen peas
- ½ cup heavy cream or coconut milk
- ½ cup parsley
- ½ cup water

1. Cut the paneer into cubes and dice the onions
2. Add everything up to the peas and excluding the paneer to the instant pot, mix well
3. Pressure cook for 5min, naturally release for 5min, then quick release
4. Add all remaining ingredients and saute for 5-8min, stirring occasionally
