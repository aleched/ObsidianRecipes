#Vegetarian #InstantPot #Lentil #Curry 
- 1 cup (207g) urad dal
- 2½ cup stock or water ½ cup extra
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 2 star anise
- 1 tsp cloves
- 1 tsp whole peppercorns
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp salt, to taste
- lemon juice, to taste

1. Wash and rinse the urad dal and dice the onion
2. Saute the whole spices in the ghee using the instant pot until fragrant, 2min

13. Prepare and add pot-in-pot rice
