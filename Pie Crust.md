#Dessert #Pie #Oven 
- 150g (1¼ cup) flour
- 85g (¾ cup) grated cheddar
- 113g (8 tbsp) butter, very cold
- 2-4 tbsp ice water
- (optional) replace cheddar with 2 tbsp butter

1. Add the flour and cheese to the food processor, pulse until coarse crumbs form
2. Add butter cut into chunks, pulse until bean-size pieces form
3. Add water 1 tbsp at a time and pulse until the it comes together, moist but not wet
4. Turn dough out onto a lightly floured surface and gather into a ball
5. Flatten into a disk with the heel of your hand
6. Wrap in plastic and refrigerate 1 hour or up to 2 days, or freeze up to 3 months
#
1. Preheat the oven to 425° with a baking sheet on the middle rack
2. Roll out the dough on lightly floured silicone mat
3. Transfer to a pie tin and pierce the bottom with a fork
4. Line the crust with two sheets of aluminum foil and fill with dried beans
5. Freeze for 10min
6. Place pie crust on the baking sheet and reduce oven temp to 400°
7. Bake for 20-30min or until the crust is golden
