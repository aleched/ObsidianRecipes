#Dessert #Oven 
- 1 cup butter
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1½ cup (180g) flour
- 2 cup oats
- ½ cup raisins
- ½ cup chocolate chips

1. Preheat oven to 325° and lightly grease a 9x13 pan
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugars, mixing very well
3. Add the vanilla and salt and mix
4. Add the eggs one at a time and mix minimally
5. Add the baking soda and baking powder and mix minimally
6. Add the flour and oats and mix minimally until just combined
7. Add the raisins and chocolate chips
8. Spread into the pan and bake for 30-35min, until golden brown
