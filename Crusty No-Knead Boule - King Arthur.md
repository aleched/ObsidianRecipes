#Bread #Oven 
- 600g (5 cup) flour
- 9g (1 tbsp) instant yeast
- 12g (1 tbsp) salt
- 454g (2 cup) water, lukewarm
- Several drops of vinegar or lemon juice

1. Add the flour, salt, and yeast to mixing bowl and whisk until homogenous
2. Add the water and lemon juice, beat at medium speed for 30sec
3. Cover the bowl and let rest at room temp for 2 hours (skip if pressed for time)
4. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or for up to about 7 days (more time = more tang)
#
1. Oil and lightly flour a tall second bowl
2. Oil hands and scrape the dough out of the first bowl
3. Envelope fold the dough to get good surface tension
4. Shape the dough into a rough ball and plop it into the second bowl
5. Sift a light coating of flour over the dough and cover the bowl
6. Prove the dough for 90min before starting the next step
#
1. Put the dutch oven into the cold oven and set temp to 450°, let warm for 30min
2. Remove hot pot from oven and lightly coat inside with oil and cornmeal
3. Carefully slide the dough into the pot and score the top
4. Cover the pot and put back into the oven
5. Bake for 25-30min, remove the lid and bake for 5-10min more until loaf is browned
6. Remove the loaf from the oven and let it cool completely on a rack before slicing
