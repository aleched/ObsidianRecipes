#Chicken #Soup #Range 
- 1 full chicken or 4 bone-in thighs
- 2 carrots
- 1 leek
- ½ onion
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup lemon juice
- 1½ cup rice

1. Trim and wash the leek and cut it in half lengthwise, cut the carrots in half, and quarter the onion
2. Add the chicken, carrots, leek, and onion to the stock pot with enough water to cover
3. Bring the pot to a boil and simmer until the chicken is cooked thru and tender, 45-60min
#
1. Remove the chicken and let it cool on a tray
2. Discard carrot, leek, onion, and chicken giblets
3. Continue to simmer the broth over medium-high heat until reduced by about half, 45min
4. When the chicken is cooled, remove and discard the skin and remove and reserve one breast for another use
5. Shred the remaining meat from the thighs, legs, and other breast
#
1. Once the broth is reduced, reduce heat to medium
2. Whisk together the eggs and lemon juice in a medium bowl
3. Slowly whisk 1 cup of the hot broth into the eggs to temper them
4. Add the rice and salt to taste and cook 10min, stirring occasionally
5. Add the shredded chicken and continue to cook 10min until rice is tender, stirring occasionally
6. Slowly stir in the tempered egg mixture
7. Reduce heat to medium-low and cook 5min, stirring occasionally
8. The soup is done when it begins to thicken and look silky
