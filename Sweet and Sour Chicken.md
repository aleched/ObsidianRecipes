#Range #Chicken 
Chicken:
- 1lb of chicken thigh or breast (Cut into Stir Fry sized pieces)
- 1½ tsp of corn starch
- ½ tsp of baking soda
- 2 tsp of sugar
- ½ tsp of kosher salt
- 1 tsp of Shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp of light soy sauce
- 1 egg white

Mix vigorously and let stand for at least 15 mins

**Vegetables**
- 1 onion
- 1 green bell pepper
- 1 red bell pepper
- 10oz can of pineapple (Save liquid for sauce)
- Large dice the onion, green, red bell peppers, and pineapple (place pineapple is separate bowl)

**Aromatics**
- 4 cloves of garlic
- ½ finger of ginger

Crush in mortar and pestle.

**Sauce**
- Pineapple juice from 10oz can
- 3 tbsp of ketchup
- 2 tbsp of white vinegar
- 3½ tsp of sugar
- 1 tsp of soy sauce
- splash of shaoxing wine
- 2 tsp of corn starch

Whisk to combine

**Velveting the chicken**
1. Bring 1qt of water to a simmer in wok.
2. Once simmering add chicken one piece at a time.
3. Cook for 30-60 Seconds.
4. Remove to sheet tray in a single layer to dry.

**Stir Fry**
1. Clean wok from velveting, dry the wok and place it back on the heat.
2. Add a tiny amount of oil and rub into wok, once smoking the wok is preheated.
3. Add a couple of tablespoons of oil and swirl to coat wok.
4. Add onion and the green and red bell peppers, until tender crisp
5. Push to one side, add a little more oil and add aromatics.
6. Mix and let stir fry for about 30 seconds.
7. Add chicken, pineapple, some cashews (cashews optional) and the sauce
8. Let cook a little bit until desired thickness is reached.
