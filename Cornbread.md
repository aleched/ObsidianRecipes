#Bread #Oven 
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup frozen corn, thawed
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¾ cup yogurt
- ½ cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 3 tbsp ghee
- 2 tbsp oil

1. Put the cast iron pan into the cold oven and set temp to 425°, let warm for 30min
2. Add dry ingredients to a bowl and whisk together
3. Add wet ingredients to a separate bowl and whisk together
4. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and whisk until combined
5. Remove hot pan from oven and coat inside with 1 tbsp ghee
6. Pour in batter, shake to settle evenly, and put back in oven
7. Bake until golden brown, 20-25min
