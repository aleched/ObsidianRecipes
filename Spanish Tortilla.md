#Breakfast #Vegetarian #Range 
- ¾ cup olive oil
- 2 yukon gold potatoes
- ½ onion
- ½ cup peas (optional)
- ½ bell pepper (optional)
- 4 eggs
- salt
- pepper

1. Start heating the olive oil in the cast iron skillet
2. Cut the potatoes into quarters lengthwise, then slice them into thin wedges
3. Pour the hot oil out of the skillet over the potatoes in a bowl and stir to evenly coat
4. Pour the oil and potatoes back into the pan and let fry 5min
5. Cut the onion into thin wedges and mix them into the pan with the potatoes
6. Season with salt and pepper and add the optional ingredients
7. Lightly whisk the eggs together and season with salt and pepper
8. Once the potatoes are cooked thru, dump the skillet into the bowl with the eggs
9. Reheat the skillet while continually stirring the egg mixture
10. Once the skillet is hot enough to sizzle when egg is added, add it back to the skillet
11. Cover the skillet with a lid to help the top cook a bit
12. Once the mixture is firm enough, loosen the edges with a thin spatula
13. Flip it out onto a pot lid and then slide it back in to cook the other side
14. Cook for a few minutes, then repeat the flip and slide several times to shape it
