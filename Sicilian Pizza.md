#Pizza #Vegetarian 
**Poolish**
- 175g (¾ cup) water
- 175g (1½ cup) flour
- 1g (¼ tsp) yeast
- 1 pinch salt

**Dough**
- 325g (2¾ cup) flour
- 14g (1 tbsp ) salt
- 4g (1 tsp) yeast
- 20g (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 150g (⅔ cup) water
- 350g poolish

**Sauce**
- 20g (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 45g (4 tbsp) garlic
- 1 tbsp oregano
- 2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 28oz can crushed or whole peeled tomatoes
- 1 tsp sugar

**Toppings**
- 1 lb mozzarella cheese, sliced deli-style
- ¾ lb pepperoni or cooked sausage, sliced thin
- ¼ lb pecorino romano cheese, grated
- ¼ cup olive oil (to coat the pan)

1. Mix the poolish in a bowl until no dry flour is left, cover and rest for 6-12 hours
#
1. Add flour, salt, yeast, and olive oil to the mixing bowl and whisk to combine
2. Add the water and use dough hook to mix low until incorporated
3. Add the poolish and increase speed to medium
4. Mix until dough is stretchy and smooth, 6min
5. Pour ¼ cup olive oil into a baking sheet and spread over entire inner surface
6. Transfer dough to baking sheet and turn in oil until thoroughly coated
7. Gently spread with your hands, it will not yet stretch to fill the pan
8. Cover the baking sheet with plastic wrap and rest, 2-3 hours
#
1. Remove plastic wrap and oil hands
2. Gently push and stretch dough into the corners by pressing out from the center
3. Preheat oven to 550° and rest dough 20-30min while making the sauce
#
1. Heat olive oil in saucier over medium heat until shimmering
2. Add the garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes and cook until aromatic, 1min
3. Add the tomatoes and break them up into chunks using a pastry cutter
4. Stir in the sugar and bring to a low simmer for 15min
5. Season to taste with salt, then remove from heat
#
1. Shingle slices of mozzarella cheese evenly over surface of the dough
2. Spoon sauce on top of cheese (not all the sauce will be used)
3. Spread pepperoni slices evenly over surface
4. Sprinkle with half the pecorino cheese
5. Bake on bottom rack until bottom of pizza is golden brown, 10min
6. Remove the pizza from the oven and sprinkle with the remaining pecorino cheese
