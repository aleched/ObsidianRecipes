#Dessert #Cake #Oven 
**Cake**
- 4 eggs
- ¾ cup vegetable oil
- 1½ cup white sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 300g (2½ cup) flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 3 cup grated carrots

**Frosting**
- 4 tbsp butter, softened
- 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
- 227g  (2 cup) powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

1. Preheat the oven to 350° and grease and flour a 9x13 pan
2. Shred the carrots
3. Add the eggs, oil, sugar, and vanilla extract to the mixing bowl and beat until smooth
4. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon in a small bowl
5. Add the flour mixture and mix minimally until just combined
6. Fold in the carrots
7. Pour into prepared pan
8. Bake 40-50min, until the center of the cake is done
9. Let cool in pan for 10min, then transfer to a wire rack
#
1. Add all the frosting ingredients to the mixing bowl and whisk until fluffy
2. Spread frosting over completely cooled cake
