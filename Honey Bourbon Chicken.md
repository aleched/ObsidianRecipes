#Chicken #InstantPot #Curry 
- 900g chicken thighs or breasts
- 1 onion
- 3 gloves garlic
- 4 cup frozen stirfry vegetables
- ¼ cup honey
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- ¼ cup bourbon
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp pepper
- 2 tbsp cornstarch

1. Cut the chicken into small chunks, dice the onion, and mince the garlic
2. Use the instant pot to saute the onion in oil with paprika and tomato paste until tender, 3-5min
3. Add the garlic and stir, then turn off the instant pot
4. Add the rest of the ingredients except the cornstarch, mix well
5. Pressure cook for 7min, naturally release for 10-15min, then quick release
6. Remove some liquid from the pot and make a slurry with the cornstarch, then add back to the pot
7. Saute the sauce for a couple minutes until thickened
