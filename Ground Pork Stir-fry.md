#Pork #Range
- ½ lb ground pork
- ½ lb (1¾ cup) green beans, broccoli, peas, etc
- ¼ cup rice wine vinegar
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 5 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated

**Marinade**
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

1. Combine the pork and marinade ingredients in a bowl and mix well, cover and refrigerate 20min
2. Dice the chosen veggie into ¼-inch pieces
3. Stir-fry the pork until it begins to brown, then add the garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant
4. Add veggie, vinegar, and soy sauce and cook until softened but not mushy
5. Serve over rice
