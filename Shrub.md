#Drink 
- 450-455g fruit
- 1 knob ginger, grated
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1½ cup apple cider vinegar

1. Make a simple syrup in a saucepan over low heat
2. Measure out the fruit, then slice or gently mash it
3. Add the fruit and ginger to the pot and bring to a low simmer
4. Simmer until done, 30min
5. Stir in the vinegar and bring the mixture to a simmer
6. Strain out the fruit and pour the shrub into a jar
