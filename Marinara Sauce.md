#Sauce #Vegetarian
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves
- ½ onion, minced
- 28 ounces crushed tomatoes

1. Saute onion and garlic in olive oil over medium heat
2. Season with salt and pepper, and optionally dried italian seasonings and/or red pepper flake
3. When the onions have turned translucent, add the crushed tomatoes
4. Simmer for a few minutes and salt to taste
