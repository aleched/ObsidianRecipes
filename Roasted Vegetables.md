#Vegetarian #Side #Oven 
- Brussel sprouts
- Carrots
- Potatoes
- Squash
- Olive oil
- Seasoning

**Glaze**
- 1 Part Sweet: sugar, honey, fruit juices, mirin, etc
- 1 Part Sour: vinegar and/or citrus juice, hot sauce, etc
- 1/4 Part Salty: soy sauce, bacon bits, anchovies, minced olives or capers, etc
- 1/4 Part Umami: fish sauce, Worcestershire sauce, Maggi sauce, Miso paste, etc
- 1/8 Part Aromatic or Spicy: grated garlic/ginger, diced chilies, sriracha, etc
- Optional thickeners: cornstarch slurry (optional) or cold butter

1. Preheat oven to convection 400° with the baking sheet inside
2. Wash and cut the vegetables, or pull them out of the freezer
3. Toss them in olive oil and space them out on a baking rack
4. Pull the sheet out, put the baking rack on it, and quickly put it back on the bottom oven rack
5. Cook for 20-30min, until lightly charred
6. Toss in a bowl with olive oil and seasoning or glaze
