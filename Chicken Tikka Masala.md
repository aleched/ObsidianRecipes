#Chicken #Curry #InstantPot 
**Marinade**
- 2 chicken breasts
- ½ cup greek yogurt
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp ginger
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp cumin
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp liquid smoke

**Sauce**
- 1 14oz can tomatoes
- 1 onion
- 1 carrot
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp ginger
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cumin
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp garam masala

1. Add the marinade ingredients to a bowl, mix well
2. Add the chicken and mix well to coat, cover and refrigerate overnight
#
1. Add all the sauce ingredients up to the frozen vegetables to the instant pot, mix well
2. Add the chicken and marinade to the top
3. Pressure cook for 10min, then quick release
4. Remove the chicken and set aside
5. Use immersion blender to puree the sauce
6. Add the frozen vegetables, heavy cream, and garam masala, mix well
7. Cut the chicken into chunks and add back
