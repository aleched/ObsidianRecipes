#Side #Vegetarian 
- 6 parts roasted corn kernels
- 1 part creamy: mayo and/or Mexican crema (try a 50/50 blend)
- 1/2 part fresh aromatics: diced onion and optionally chopped cilantro, green onion, or minced garlic
- Salt & lime juice, to taste
- Garnish with crumbled cheese, paprika, chile powder, or chile flakes

1. Char the corn kernels: If using fresh corn cobs (recommended), char over a grill, flame, or broiler, and then cut toasted kernels off the cob. Alternatively, toast kernels in a ripping hot pan with some oil until they take on some color. Allow the corn kernels to cool.
2. Mix together & serve: Combine all Elote Components in a bowl or serving vessel, adding salt and more lime juice until well seasoned. Add more mayo or crema if you prefer a creamier corn salad. Top or serve with garnishes of choice on the side.
