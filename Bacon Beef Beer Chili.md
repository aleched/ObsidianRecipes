#Beef #Chili #Range 
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ¼ lb bacon
- 1 onion
- 1 green pepper
- 3 stalks celery
- 2 15 oz cans of crushed fire roasted tomatoes
- 2 15 oz cans of kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp cocoa powder
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 1 12 oz bottle of beer (use a dark beer best results)

1. Mix the baking soda into the beef
2. Dice the bacon, onion, pepper, celery, and garlic
3. Cook the bacon in the pot
4. Add the onion to the pot and cook until softened
5. Add the beef and cook until browned
6. Add the rest of the ingredients to the pot and mix well
7. Bring the chili to a gentle boil, stirring occasionally
8. Cover the pot, reduce heat and simmer for 1 hour
