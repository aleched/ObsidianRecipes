#Soup #InstantPot #Vegetarian #Chicken 
- 2 tsp oil
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp basil
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 qt chicken broth
- 2 14oz cans diced tomatoes
- 1 chicken breast or 2 sausages
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 4 cup tortellini
- 200g (3 cup) spinach
- ½ cup parmesan cheese
- 1 cup cream

1. Dice the onion and use the instant pot to saute it in the oil
2. Add everything up to the tortellini, mix
3. Pressure cook 10min, then quick release
4. Remove the chicken or sausages, cut into chunks, then add back to the pot
5. Add the rest of the ingredients and saute to heat through
