#Breakfast #Vegetarian #Range 
- 300g (2½ cup) flour
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- 1½ cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp butter, melted

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the wet ingredients
3. Whisk the wet into the dry ingredients until just combined
4. Rest for 5min, then cook
