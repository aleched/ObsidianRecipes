#Soup #InstantPot #Vegetarian 
- ½ lb bacon or mushrooms
- 1 onion
- 1 kg sweet potatoes
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 3 cup chicken stock
- 1 tsp pepper
- 3 cup frozen corn
- 1½ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp flour
- ½ cup heavy cream

1. Cut the bacon into matchsticks, dice the onion, and the potatoes into chunks
2. Use the instant pot to saute the bacon until crispy
3. Add the onion, garlic, and parsley and cook until the onions are tender, 3-5min
4. Add the potatoes, chicken stock, and pepper
5. Pressure cook for 7min, naturally release for 10min, then quick release
6. Use immersion blender to slightly puree the soup, still leaving lots of chunks
7. Add corn and salt, mix well
8. Whisk together flour and half of the cream in a small bowl, then add to the pot
9. Saute until thickened, stirring constantly
10. Turn off the pot, add the rest of the cream, mix well
