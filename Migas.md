#Breakfast #Vegetarian #Range 
- Eggs
- Shredded Cheese
- Salt
- Onion, diced
- Pepper, diced
- Tomato, diced
- Tortilla chips, crushed

1. Whisk the eggs with salt
2. Sauté the onion and pepper until soft
3. Drain the tomato and sauté
4. Add the eggs and cheese and chips, cook until the eggs are set
5. Serve on warmed corn tortillas with hot sauce, avocado, cilantro, raw onion
