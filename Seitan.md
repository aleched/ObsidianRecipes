#Range #Vegetarian 
- 110g (¾ cup) vital wheat gluten
- 30g (¼ cup) flour
- 1 tsp ranch powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp MSG
- ¼ tsp salt
- 150g (⅔ cup) water

1. Add an inch of water to the stock pot with the steamer basket and bring to a light boil
2. Add everything but the water to a medium bowl and mix well
3. Add the water and knead the mixture into a ball of dough
4. Tear the dough into chunks and place them in a single layer in the steamer basket
5. Cover and steam for 10min
6. Pan fry the steamed seitan in oil until they are golden brown and crispy
