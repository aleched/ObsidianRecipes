#Dessert #IceCream
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 cup heavy cream
- 2eggs
- ¼-¾ cup sugar

1. Crack the eggs into a small bowl and whisk in the sugar
2. Add the milk to the saucier along with any dry mix-ins, heat low and whisk until smooth
3. Add the cream and continue heating and whisking until it reaches 140°
4. Slowly whisk 1 cup of the cream mix into the eggs to temper them
5. Pour the egg mix into the saucier and whisk together
6. Continue heating and whisking until it reaches 170°
7. Immediately pour the mix into the small bowl, cover, and chill at least 3 hours
8. Delicate mix-ins such as vanilla extract and cinnamon should be added after cooled
