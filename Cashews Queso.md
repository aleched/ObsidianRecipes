#Side #Vegetarian 
- 150 g (~1 cup) raw cashews
- 1 clove of garlic
- 6 g (1 tsp) nutritional yeast
- ​6 g (1 tsp) chili powder
- ​3 g (½ tsp) ground cumin
- 3 g (½ tsp) salt, plus more to taste
- 15 g (1 tbsp) harissa (or sub hot salsa, hot sauce, Ro-Tel, or 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce, plus more for garnish)
- Hot water, for blending

1. Add all ingredients to a small blender (starting with a small amount of water) and blend until creamy, adding more water as needed.
2. Add just enough water to achieve a smooth, pourable queso.
3. If it gets too thin, thicken it with additional raw cashews.
4. Taste and adjust flavor as needed, adding more nutritional yeast for cheesiness, salt to taste, cumin for smokiness, chili powder or harissa for heat, or garlic for zing.
5. Store leftovers covered in the refrigerator for up to 5-7 days, or in the freezer for up to 1 month.
6. Reheat in the microwave or in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add more water as needed when reheating.
