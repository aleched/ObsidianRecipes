#Bread #Oven 
- 418g (3½ cups) flour
- 340g (1½ cup) lukewarm water (105°)
- 35g (3 tbsp) olive oil
- 9g (1 tbsp) instant yeast
- 8g (2½ tsp) salt
**Optional mix-ins**
- 1 cup cheese (crumbled feta, diced cheddar, grated parmesan)

1. Lightly spray a 9x13 pan with oil, then drizzle olive oil atop the spray
2. Add all the ingredients to the mixing bowl and beat at high speed for 1min
3. Scrape the dough into the pan and smooth it out, cover and oven prove low for 60min
#
1. Preheat the oven to 350°
2. Drizzle dough with olive oil and sprinkle desired seasonings
3. Bake for 35-40min, until golden brown
4. Rest in the pan for 5min, then turn it out onto a rack
