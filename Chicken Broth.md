#InstantPot #Chicken #Soup 
- 1 chicken carcass
- 10 cup cold water
- 1 onion
- 2 carrots
- 3 celery stalks
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp whole peppercorns
- 1 tsp thyme
- 3 bay leaves

1. Roast the chicken carcass
2. Roughly chop the chicken carcass and vegetables
3. Add the chicken and water to the instant pot
4. Pressure cook for 240min, then naturally release
5. Add the vegetables and aromatics
6. Pressure cook for 30min, then naturally release
7. Strain broth and transfer to storage containers when cooled
8. Refrigerate up to 7 days, or freeze up to 3 months
