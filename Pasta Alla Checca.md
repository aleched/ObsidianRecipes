#Noodles #Vegetarian #Range 
- ½ lb pasta (spaghetti, udon, etc)
- 1 lb fresh tomatoes
- 1 tbsp basil
- 1 tbsp oregano
- 1 tbsp thyme
- 45g olive oil
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 4oz fresh mozzarella

1. Set a kettle of water on to boil
2. Add the pasta to the sauté pan with a large pinch of salt
3. Once the water has boiled, pour it over top of the pasta until completely submerged at least 1cm
4. Set the heat on high and cook until al dente, stirring occasionally to avoid sticking
5. Dice the tomatoes and add them to the serving bowl with a sprinkle of salt and roughly mix
6. Add the basil, oregano, and thyme, letting them hydrate but keeping them somewhat mounded
7. Add the olive oil to a small pan over medium heat, when it starts to smoke remove from heat
8. Pour off the excess tomato liquid and add the black pepper
9. Pour the hot olive oil over the herb mound and mix thoroughly
10. Once the pasta is done, use the spider to add it to the bowl and toss until combined
11. Add a little bit of the pasta water if needed to create the emulsion
12. Add the mozzarella balls and cover the bowl for 1min to let them melt a bit before serving
