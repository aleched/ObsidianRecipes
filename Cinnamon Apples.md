#Dessert #InstantPot 
- 6 granny smith apples
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp nutmeg
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ water
- 2 tbsp cornstarch

1. Slice and core the apples
2. Add everything but the water and cornstarch to the instant pot and mix
3. Whisk the water and cornstarch together in a small bowl, add to the instant pot and mix
4. Pressure cook for 7min, naturally release for 10min, then quick release
5. Stir to coat the apples in sauce and serve
