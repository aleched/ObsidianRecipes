#Pork #Oven 
- 1 whole 8 lb pork butt
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup kosher salt
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1 tbsp kosher salt

1. Mix the sugar and salt in a bowl
2. Put the pork shoulder in a roasting pan and rub the mixture into the meat
3. Cover the pan with plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight
#
1. Preheat the oven to 300º
2. Remove the pork from the refrigerator and discard any juices
3. Cook for 6 hours, basting every hour
4. Remove pork and let rest for up to 1 hour
5. When ready to serve, heat the oven to convection 450º
6. Mix the brown sugar and salt and rub over the pork
7. Cook for 10-15 minutes, until the sugar has melted into a crust
