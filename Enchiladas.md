#Cassarole #Vegetarian #Chicken 
**Sauce**
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp flour
- ¼ cup chili powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp cumin
- ¼ tsp oregano
- 2 cup chicken stock
- ½ cup cream

**Enchiladas**
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion
- 1 chicken breast
- 1 4oz can diced green chiles
- 1 15oz can black beans
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 8-12 flour tortillas
- 3+ cup shredded cheese

1. Heat oil over medium-high heat, add flour and cook for 1min, mix well
2. Add the chili powder, paprika, cumin, and oregano and cook for 1min, mix well
3. Add the stock and simmer 10-15min until thickened, mix well
4. Remove from heat and add the cream, mix well
5. Preheat oven to 350°
6. Dice the onion, cut the chicken breast into chunks, drain and rinse the beans
7. Sauté the onion in the oil for 3min
8. Add the chicken and green chilis, season generously, and cook until not pink
9. Remove from heat and add the beans and corn, mix well
10. In each tortilla add 2 tbsp sauce, chicken mix, and ⅓ cup cheese
11. Roll each tortilla into a 9x13 pan, add the remaining sauce and more cheese
12. Bake uncovered for 20min
