#Vegetarian #Curry #InstantPot #Lentil 
- ½ cup moong dal
- 1 tbsp ghee
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- ½ cup onion
- 2 tsp ginger
- 3 cloves garlic
- ½ cup tomato
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ¼ tsp garam masala
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- 2 cup water

1. Rinse the dal 2-3 times and soak 15min
2. Dice the onion and tomato and mince the garlic
3. Use the instant pot to saute the cumin seeds in the ghee until they sizzle, 30sec
4. Add the onion and saute until they soften, 2-3min
5. Add the ginger and garlic and saute until fragrant, 1min
6. Add the tomato, salt, turmeric, garam masala, and cayenne and stir
7. Cook until tomatoes soften, 2min
8. Gently mash the tomatoes using the back of your spoon
9. Drain the dal and add to the pot with the water and stir
10. Pressure cook for 3min, naturally release for 5min, then quick release
11. Stir the dal, mashing some against the walls of the pot to thicken it
12. Serve over rice, garnished with cilantro and a few drops of lemon juice
