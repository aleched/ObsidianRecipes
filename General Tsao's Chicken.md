#Chicken #Fry 
- 700g chicken thighs
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 cup cornstarch

**Sauce**
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp green onion, diced
- 1 strip orange zest
- ½ tsp cayenne
- ½ cup white sugar
- ¼ tsp ground ginger
- 3 tbsp chicken broth
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp peanut oil
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- ¼ cup water

1. Heat oil in the dutch oven to 375°
2. Cut the thicken into small chunks
3. Beat the egg in a medium bowl then add the chicken, salt, sugar, pepper and mix well
4. Mix in the cornstarch slowly to avoid clumping until the chicken is well coated
5. Drop the chicken into the oil one by one and fry until all sides are light brown, 3min
6. Drain on a paper towel on a wire rack set over a baking sheet
7. Reheat the oil and repeat with the additional batches of chicken if necessary
8. Reheat the oil and fry a second time until all sides are golden brown, 2min
9. Drain and repeat with the additional batches
10. Heat the oil in the saute pan, then add the garlic, green onion, orange zest, and cayenne and saute 30sec
11. Add the rest of the sauce ingredients except the cornstarch, bring to a boil and cook for 3min
12. Make a slurry with the cornstarch and water and add to the pan
13. Cook the sauce for a couple minutes until thickened
14. Add the chicken and reduce heat to low
15. Cook for a few minutes until the chicken absorbs some of the sauce
