#Dessert #Pie #Oven 
- 1 pie crust
- 8 granny smith apples
- 8 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp flour
- ¼ cup water
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar

1. Preheat oven to 425°
2. Peel, core, and slice the apples
3. Melt the butter in the saucier, then whisk in the flour to form a paste
4. Add the water and sugars, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer
5. Place the bottom crust in the pan and fill it with the apple slices
6. Cover with a lattice crust
7. Slowly pour the liquid mixture over the crust
8. Bake for 15min, then reduce the temp to 350° and continue baking for 35-45min
