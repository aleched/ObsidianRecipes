#Dessert #Bar #Oven 
- 6 granny smith apples
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2 egg whites
- Powdered sugar

1. Preheat oven to 350°
2. Wash the apples and place them on the quarter sheet pan with ¼ inch of water
3. Roast the apples 1 hour, until golden, saggy, and wrinkly
#
1. Remove the apples from the oven and allow them to cool completely
2. Scrape the skins and cores to get a soft, seed-free puree
3. Blend the puree until smooth
4. Whisk the puree, sugar, and egg whites in the stand mixer, scraping down the sides occasionally, 10min
5. Preheat oven to 170°
6. Line a baking sheet, including the sides, with parchment paper
7. The puree will have nearly quadrupled and the a bowl should be filled with gleaming, thick white foam
8. Reserve ~1½ cup of the puree in a container in the fridge
9. Scrape the rest of the puree into the pan and spread it in an even layer
10. Bake for 4-6 hours, until it is dry to the touch and solid enough to pick up without flopping
#
1. Remove the pastila from the oven and let it cool completely before peeling away the parchment paper
2. Use a knife dipped in hot water to cut the pastila into three fat rectangles
3. Stack the three pieces and use the reserved puree as glue and to fill any holes
4. Bake the stacked pastila on fresh parchment paper until the layers have all molded together, 90min
5. Remove the pastila from the oven and let it cool
6. Carefully cut the pastila into fingers, then rub it all over with powdered sugar
