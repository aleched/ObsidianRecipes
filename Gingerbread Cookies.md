- 145g (10 tbsp) butter
- 150g (¾ cup) brown sugar
- 200g (⅔ cup) molasses (unsulphured or dark, not blackstrap, prefer Grandma’s brand)
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 440g (3½ cup) flour
- 1 tbsp ground ginger
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground allspice
- ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt

1. Soften the butter and cream it with the brown sugar and molasses, mixing very well
3. Add the egg and vanilla and mix well
4. Mix the dry ingredients together separately then slowly add to the wet on low speed until just combined
6. Divide dough in half and tightly wrap in plastic
7. Press into a disc and chill overnight
#
1. Preheat oven to 350°
1. Flour the counter, roll out a disc of chilled dough until ¼ inch thick, and cut into shapes
3. Bake 8-11min, depending on their size
