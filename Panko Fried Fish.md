#Fish #Fry 
- 450-900g white fish (cod, tilapia, etc)
- 80g flour
- 40g cornstarch
- 2 eggs
- 100g panko bread crumbs

1. Heat oil in the dutch oven to 350°
2. Cut fish into evenly sized fillets and sprinkle with salt
3. Add the flour and cornstarch to one bowl, the beaten egg to a second, and the panko bread crumbs to a third
4. Coat each fillet thoroughly in the flour, then the egg, then press with the panko
5. Drop the fillets into the oil one by one and cook until golden brown, 4-6min
6. Drain on a paper towel on a wire rack set over a baking sheet, sprinkle with salt
