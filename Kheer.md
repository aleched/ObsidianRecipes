#Dessert #InstantPot 
- 2 tbsp ghee
- ¼ cup raisins
- ¼ cup cashews
- ¼ cup basmati rice, rinsed
- 1¾ cup milk
- ¼ cup heavy cream
- ¼ cup sugar
- ½ cup water
- ½ cup heavy cream
- ½ tsp cinnamon

1. Use the instant pot to saute the raisins and cashews in the ghee until lightly browned and plumped
2. Add the rice, milk, cream, sugar, and water
3. Pressure cook for 15min, then naturally release
4. Mix well while slowly adding the rest of the cream until smooth
5. Add the cinnamon and chill overnight before serving for best results
