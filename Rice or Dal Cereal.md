#Baby 
- Rice or Dal

1. Wash the grain until the water runs clear
2. Submerge under an inch of water and soak in an uncovered bowl for 6 hours
3. Drain and spread on a clean towel to air dry 1 hour
4. Dry roast over medium heat for 5min, then low heat until it begins to pop and sputter
5. Let it cool in the pan after roasting, then powder it
