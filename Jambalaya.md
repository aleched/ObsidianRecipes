#InstantPot #Chicken #Cassarole 
- 4 sausages
- 1 chicken breast
- 1 onion
- 1 green pepper
- 3 stalks celery
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ginger powder
- 1 tsp basil
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- ½ tsp sugar
- 1½ cup rice
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 14oz can tomatoes
- 1¾ cup chicken broth

1. Cut the chicken and sausages into chunks
2. Roughly chop the onion, green pepper, and celery
3. Saute the sausage in oil until browned, 2-3min, then remove and set aside
4. Saute the chicken in oil until browned, 1min, then turn off pot
5. Add the onion, green pepper, celery, and garlic and cook for 1min
6. Add the seasonings and rice, mix well
7. Add the tomatoes and chicken broth
8. Pressure cook for 5min, naturally release for 5 minutes, then quick release
9. Fluff the rice and add the sausage, then cover and rest for 5min
