#Drink
*Makes ~8 cups*
- 8 eggs
- 300g sugar
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 2 cup whole milk
- 2 cup heavy cream
- ¾ cup Jamaican rum
- ¾ cup brandy
- ½ cup bourbon
- 1 pinch kosher salt

1. Separate the eggs and store the whites for another application
2. Add the yolks, sugar, and nutmeg to the stand mixer
3. Whisk until the mixture turns pale yellow and falls off the whisk in a solid ribbon
4. Combine the rest of the ingredients in second large bowl
5. Slowly whisk the alcoholic dairy into the egg mixture, scraping the bottom midway
6. Transfer the eggnog to a large glass jar and store in the fridge for 2 weeks to 2 months
