- 4 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1-3 tbsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp vanilla
- 1½ cup almond or coconut milk
- ½ cup chia seeds

1. Add the dry ingredients to a deli container and mix
2. Add milk a little at a time and mix until a paste forms
3. Add the remaining milk and mix until smooth
4. Add the chia seeds and mix until combined
5. Cover and refrigerate overnight
