#Side #Vegetarian #Oven 
- Yukon Gold potatoes
- Fat (melted butter, olive oil, coconut oil, bacon fat, etc)

1. Preheat oven to 425°
2. Wash and dry the potatoes
3. Cut parallel slits in the potatoes ⅛ inch apart, leaving the bottom intact so that the slices stay connected
4. Arrange the potatoes in a baking dish and brush all over with the fat, including the bottoms
5. Season generously with salt and pepper
6. Cook for 30min, then brush with more fat, making sure some gets down between the slices
7. Cook until the edges are crispy and the middle is soft, 30-40min
