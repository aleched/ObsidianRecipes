#Chicken #InstantPot #Curry 
- 1 onion
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 cup chicken stock
- ¼ cup lemon juice
- 2 chicken breasts
- 3 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp cold water
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp salt

1. Peel and dice the onion
2. Use the instant pot to saute the onion in oil with paprika and tomato paste until tender, 3-5min
3. Add garlic and cook for 1min, then turn off the pot
4. Add chicken stock, lemon juice, and chicken
5. Pressure cook for 10min (+2min if frozen)
6. Naturally release for 10min, then quick release
7. Remove the chicken and set aside
8. Whisk together flour and water in a small bowl, then add to the pot
9. Saute until thickened, stirring constantly
10. Turn off the pot, add heavy cream, salt, and chicken and mix
