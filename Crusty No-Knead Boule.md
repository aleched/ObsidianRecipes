#Bread #Oven 
- 400g (3⅓ cup) flour
- 8g (2 tsp) gluten
- 4g (1 tsp) yeast
- 8g salt (2½ tsp) salt
- 270g (1 cup) water, lukewarm
- Several drops of vinegar or lemon juice

1. Add the dry ingredients to a large bowl and hand mix until homogeneous
2. Add the wet ingredients to a small bowl, then add the wet to the dry
3. Hand mix until the dough is a sticky, shaggy, single mass, 30-45sec
4. Cover and let the dough rest for 20min
5. Reach under the dough then stretch and fold it into the center, rotate and repeat for the other 3 sides
6. Repeat the 20min rest followed by the stretch and fold 3-4 more times
7. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or for up to about 7 days (more time = more tang)
#
1. Remove the dough from the fridge and let it come up to room temp, 1-8hours
#
1. Oil and lightly flour a medium bowl
2. Flour the work surface, then tilt the large bowl over it and flour the inside
3. Use a floured scraper and fingers to gently tip the dough out
4. With floured hands, quickly and gently do one last stretch and fold
5. Use the bench scraper to gently flip the dough
6. Rotate and tuck it together underneath, until the top is smooth and taut
7. Place it seam-side down into the medium bowl and dust with flour
8. Cover the bowl with a baking sheet and prove 90min before starting the next step
#
1. Preheat the oven to 500° for 30min
2. Invert the bowl and baking sheet so the dough is on the sheet
3. Remove the bowl, splash some water inside the large bowl, then invert it over the dough
4. Transfer to the oven, reduce the temp to 450°, and bake for 20min
5. Remove the bowl and bake 15-20min more until loaf is browned
6. Remove the loaf from the oven and let it cool completely on a rack before slicing
