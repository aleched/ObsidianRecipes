#Noodles #Vegetarian #Oven 
- 1 lb italian sausage
- ½ lb ground beef
- 1 lb ziti or penne pasta
- 1 onion
- 5 cloves garlic
- 1 large can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 14oz (400g) mozzarella, low skim
- 4oz (113g) Parmigiano/Pecorino
- 15oz (425g) tub full fat ricotta

1. Dice the onion, grate the cheeses, and remove the sausage casings
2. Preheat the oven to 375 and spray a 9x13 dish
3. Fill a pot with water, season with salt, and bring to a boil
4. Add the pasta and cook until under al dente
5. Heat the saute pan over med-high heat and coat with olive oil
6. Add the meat and sear for 2-3min, then flip and sear the other side
7. Break up the meat and cook until browned, then remove and set aside
8. Add the onion, salt it, and saute for 3min
9. Add the garlic and saute for 20sec
10. Add the meat back, add the tomatoes and sugar, and stir together
11. Let the meat sauce simmer for 5min
12. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and stir together
13. Add half of the pasta and sauce to the 9x13 dish and spread evenly
14. Add half the tub of ricotta and half of the cheese mixture
15. Add the other half of the pasta and sauce
16. Top with the other half of ricotta and cheese mixture
17. Bake for 35min and let cool before serving
