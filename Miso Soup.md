#Vegetarian #Soup #Tofu 
- 2 tsp dashi granules
- 3 cup water
- 3 tbsp miso paste
- 1 8oz package silken tofu, diced
- 2 green onions, sliced diagonally

1. Add the dashi granules to the water in a small pot and bring it to a boil
2. Reduce heat and whisk in the miso paste
3. Stir in the tofu and green onions
4. Gently simmer 2-3min
