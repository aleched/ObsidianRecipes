#Curry #Vegetarian #Chicken #Range 
- 2 chicken breasts
- 2 onions
- 2 carrots
- 2 potatoes
- 1½ tbsp oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp ginger
- 4 cup chicken broth
- 2 cup water
- 1 apple
- 1 tbsp honey
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 full package Japanese curry roux
- 1½ tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp ketchup

1. Cut the chicken into chunks and lightly season with salt and pepper
2. Cut the onions into wedges, the carrots rangiri, and the potatoes into chunks
3. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat, and saute the onions until tender
4. Add the garlic and ginger and mix well
5. Add the chicken and cook until no longer pink
6. Add the carrots and mix well
7. Add the chicken broth and water and bring to a boil
8. Grate in the peeled apple, and add the honey and salt
9. Lower heat and simmer uncovered for 20min, stirring occasionally
10. Add the potatoes and cook until tender, 10-15min
11. Add the curry roux and dissolve on a slotted spoon
12. Add the soy sauce, fish sauce, gochujang, ketchup and mix well
13. Simmer until thick
