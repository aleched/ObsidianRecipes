#Chili #Chicken #InstantPot 
- 2 chicken breasts
- 1 15oz cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 15oz cans white beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 15oz cans of corn OR 2 cups frozen corn
- 1 15oz cans of diced tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp powdered ranch seasoning
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp Lime juice
- ½ tbsp salt
- ½ tbsp pepper
- ½ tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp liquid smoke
- 1 8oz package cream cheese, cut into chunks

1. Add ingredients to the instant pot in the order listed
2. Mix well, but leave the chicken at the bottom
3. Cook 20min, wait 10min, then quick release
4. Remove chicken breasts, shred, add back to pot
5. Mix to combine ingredients and melt cheese
