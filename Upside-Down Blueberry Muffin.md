#Breakfast #Dessert #Oven #Vegetarian 
**Batter**
- 340g (2⅓ cup) flour
- 145g (¾ cup) sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp coriander
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 170g (6oz) butter, melted
- 140g (½ cup) milk
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla

**Topping**
- 455g (2⅔ cups) blueberries, thawed
- 50g (¼ cup) sugar
- 45g (3 tbsp) lemon juice
- salt to taste

1. Preheat the oven to 350° and line the cast iron skillet with parchment paper
2. Add the batter ingredients to a large bowl and mix until just combined
3. Toss the topping ingredients together in a bowl, then spread into the skillet in an even layer
4. Dollop the batter over the blueberries, then smooth with a spatula
5. Bake 45min, until puffed and golden brown
6. To serve, run a knife around the edge of the pan then flip onto a plate
