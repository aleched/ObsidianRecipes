#Breakfast #Vegetarian #Range 
- ¼ cup flour
- 1 tbsp  sugar
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 eggs
- 12 thick slices bread

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the wet ingredients
3. Slowly whisk the wet into the dry ingredients until smooth
4. Soak the bread slices until saturated, cook until golden brown
