#Breakfast #Vegetarian #Range 
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- ¾ cup whole milk
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 225g (1½ cup) mochiko flour

1. Preheat the waffle iron
2. Whisk an egg in a mixing bowl until fluffy, then slowly whisk in the butter and milk
3. Whisk in sugar, baking powder, and salt
4. Whisk in the mochiko flour until a paste-like batter forms, pour it into a piping bag
5. Spray the waffle iron with oil and add enough batter to fill up the iron ¾ of the way around
6. Cook until golden brown, top with whipped cream and bananas
