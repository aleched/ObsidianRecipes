#Vegetarian #Curry #InstantPot #Lentil 
- 1 cup rice
- ½ cup lentils, small yellow/moong dal
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tbsp ginger
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 bay leaf
- 6 cup water
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp turmeric

1. Soak the rice and lentils in cold water for 15-30min, then drain and rinse
#
1. Saute the ginger, cumin, coriander, and bay in the ghee using the instant pot until fragrant, 2min
2. Add the rest of the ingredients
3. Pressure cook for 12min, naturally release for 20min, then quick release
4. Serve each bowl with a spoonful of ghee and a pinch of salt
