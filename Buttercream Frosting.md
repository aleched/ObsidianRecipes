#Dessert 
- 340g (1½ cup) butter, at room temperature
- 570g (4½ cup) powdered sugar
- 3-4 tbsp heavy cream
- 1½ tsp vanilla extract

1. Add the butter to the stand mixer and attach the side-scraping paddle
2. Whip the butter on medium-high speed until nearly white and very fluffy, 5min
3. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix on low speed until blended
4. Increase the speed to medium and beat until very light and fluffy, 5min
