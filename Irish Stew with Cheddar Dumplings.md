#Beef #Range #Oven #Cassarole 
**Dumplings**
- 180g (1½ cup) flour
- 9g (2¼ tsp) baking powder
- 3g (½ tsp) salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp thyme
- ⅓ cup coconut oil
- ¾ cup grated cheddar
- ⅔ cup milk

**Stew**
- ¼ lb bacon
- 2 lbs stewing beef
- 1 onion
- 3 carrots
- 4 celery sticks
- 2 potatoes
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 6 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 12 oz dark beer
- 2 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 1 qt beef stock
- 3 tsp thyme
- 1 bay leaf

1. Cut the beef into chunks and season with salt and pepper
2. Cut the onions into wedges, the carrots rangiri, the celery and potatoes diced
3. Heat saute pan over medium heat, add oil
4. Cook the bacon, then remove and set aside
5. Cook the beef until browned, then remove and set aside
6. Cook the onion, carrots, and celery until tender, 3-5min
7. Add the garlic and cook 1min
8. Add the tomato paste, beer, and worcestershire sauce and bring to a simmer
9. Add the beef back along with the stock, thyme, and bay
10. Cover and simmer for 90min, then add the potatoes and simmer 30min more
11. Remove the bay and add the bacon back
12. Preheat the oven to 350°
13. Mix the dry dumpling ingredients in a bowl, cut in the shortening until crumbly
14. Add the cheddar and mix, then add the milk and mix until moist
15. Shape the dumpling dough into 6 ½ inch thick patties and place on top of the stew
16. Bake uncovered until the dumplings are browned, 30-40min
