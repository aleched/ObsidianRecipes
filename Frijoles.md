#Side #InstantPot #Vegetarian 
- 450g (2½ cups) dried pinto beans
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 4 cups broth
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1½ tsp oregano
- 1½ tsp paprika
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 (14oz) can tomatoes
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped

1. Soak the beans in cold water for 15min, then drain and rinse
2. Saute the onion, garlic, and bell pepper with olive oil in the instant pot
3. Add the beans, broth, and seasonings and mix well
4. Add the tomatoes but don't mix
5. Pressure cook 50min then naturally release
6. Discard the bay leaves and stir the beans
7. Add the cilantro and more salt and seasonings to taste
8. Garnish with shredded cheese or avocado and serve
