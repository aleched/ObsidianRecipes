#Dessert #Pie #Oven 
**Crust**
- 120g (1 cup) flour
- 6 tbsp butter, very cold
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ tsp sugar
- ¼ cup water, cold

**Filling**
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 3 tbsp water
- 6 large apples (Golden Delicious, Braeburn, or Honeycrisp)
- ¼ cup sliced dried dates
- ¼ cup raisins
- ¼ cup slivered almonds
- 5 tbsp butter
- ½ cup water
- 2 tbsp sugar

1. Add the flour, salt, and sugar to the food processor with the butter cut into chunks
2. Process 5-8sec, butter should still be visible in the dough
3. Add the water and process 5-10sec, just until the dough starts gathering
4. Turn the dough out onto a large sheet of plastic wrap and form it into a loose mass
5. Cover the dough with another sheet of plastic wrap and roll it out into a circle
6. Lift the dough round onto a cookie sheet and refrigerate until ready
#
1. Add ⅓ cup sugar and 3 tbsp water to the carbon steel skillet
2. Cook over high heat until it caramelizes, 3-4min
3. Remove the skillet from the heat and let the caramel harden
4. Cut the apples into quarters and remove the cores
5. Arrange apple quarters on top of the caramel skin side down in one layer
6. Sprinkle on the dates, raisins, and nuts, and dot with the butter
7. Slice the remaining apples thin and arrange to form a flat surface
8. Add ½ cup water and bring to a boil, then cover and boil gently for 10min
9. Remove the lid and cook over medium heat for 7-8min, until there is no visible liquid
#
1. Preheat oven to 400°
2. Remove plastic wrap from dough round and arrange on top of the apples
3. Press it down gently, pierce several times with a fork, and sprinkle with 2 tbsp sugar
4. Bake for 40-45min, until golden brown
5. If any visible juices remain, cook over medium heat on the stove for 3-4min
6. Before serving, heat the skillet for a few minutes, shaking it to release the apples
7. Place a plate upside down over the skillet and invert the contents of the pan onto it
