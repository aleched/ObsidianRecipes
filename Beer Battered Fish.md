#Fish #Fry 
- 700g cod
- 190g (1¼ cup) flour
- 9g (1 tbsp) salt
- 7g (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 7g (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 egg
- 1 12oz can beer

1. Heat oil in the dutch oven to 350°
2. Cut fish into evenly sized fillets and sprinkle with salt
3. Mix the batter ingredients together until smooth
4. Toss the fillets in flour to fully coat, then shake off excess
5. Dunk the fillets in the batter and drop into the oil
6. Fry until golden brown, 7min
7. Drain on a paper towel on a wire rack set over a baking sheet, sprinkle with salt
