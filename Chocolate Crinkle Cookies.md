#Dessert #Cookie #Oven 
- ½ cup butter
- 2 cup sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- ⅔ cup (113g) unsweetened bakers chocolate
- 2⅓ cup (275g) flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ cup powdered sugar

1. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugar, mixing very well
2. While creaming, melt the chocolate in a bain marie
3. Add the eggs, vanilla, and melted chocolate and mix well
4. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in small bowl
5. Add the flour mixture to the dough and mix until the dough is cohesive
6. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour or up to 3 days
