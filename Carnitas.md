#Oven #Pork
- 3-4 lbs pork shoulder
- 3 bay leaves
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 onion, quartered
- 1 orange, quartered
- Sprinkle of cinnamon
- 1/4 - ½ cup peanut oil

1. Preheat the oven to 275°
2. Cut the pork shoulder up into ~2 inch cubes
3. Pack the pork into the saute pan in a single layer and season with salt
4. Add the other ingredients  
    Add enough peanut oil to coat the top, not swimming but filling the cracks
5. Cover and bake for 3+ hours, until the pork is just tender enough to start shredding but not dried out
6. Shred the carnitas onto an aluminum foil lined sheet tray and broil them until very crispy and brown on top
