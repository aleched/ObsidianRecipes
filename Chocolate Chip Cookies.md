#Dessert #Cookie #Oven 
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp water
- 3½ cup (440g) flour
- 1 cup chocolate chips

1. Preheat oven to 350°
2. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugars, mixing very well
3. Add the vanilla and salt and mix
4. Add the eggs one at a time and mix minimally
5. Dissolve the baking soda in the water, add and mix minimally
6. Add the flour and mix minimally until just combined
7. Add the chocolate chips
8. Spoon onto pans and bake 10min or until done
