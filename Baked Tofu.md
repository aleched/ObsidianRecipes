#Oven #Tofu #Vegetarian 
- Firm tofu
- Nutritional yeast
- No-salt seasoning

1. Preheat oven to convection 350°
2. Drain and press tofu for several minutes
3. Cut tofu block into slices
4. Pour nutritional yeast into a bowl and use it to coat each slice of tofu
5. Arrange the slices on a baking sheet (use wire rack?) and season liberally
6. Cook for 30-40min
7. Rest the tofu for at least 5 minutes before using a spatula to remove
