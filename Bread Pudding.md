#Dessert #Bar #Oven 
- 6 slices day-old bread
- 2 tbsp butter
- ½ cup raisins
- 4 eggs
- 2 cup milk
- ⅔ cup sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

1. Preheat oven to 325°
2. Break bread into chunks into an 8 inch square baking pan
3. Drizzle melted butter over bread and sprinkle with raisins
4. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla in a bowl and pour over bread
5. Push down with a fork until bread is covered and soaking up egg mixture
6. Bake 50-60min, until the top springs back when lightly tapped.
