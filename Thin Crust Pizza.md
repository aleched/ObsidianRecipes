#Pizza #Vegetarian 
**Crust for 2 pizzas**
- 300g flour
- 5g gluten
- 7.5g salt
- 7.5g sugar
- 1.5g yeast
- 150g water
- 30g olive oil

**Sauce for 2 pizzas**
- 1 (14-ounce) can tomatoes with their juices
- 85g (½ 6-ounce can) tomato paste
- 2 medium garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp dried Italian seasoning (oregano, marjoram, thyme, basil, parsley, rosemary)
- 1½ tsp granulated sugar, or to taste
- 1 tsp red-wine vinegar, or to taste
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt

**Toppings for 1 pizza**
- 340g mozzarella
- 230g sausage
- 60g Pecorino

1. Whisk together the flour, salt, sugar, and yeast in a stand mixer
2. Add the water and oil, mix until a shaggy ball is formed
3. Cover the bowl and rest for 10min
4. Knead with a dough hook for 10min
5. Divide the dough into two equal balls
6. Coat them lightly with oil and transfer to quart containers, refrigerate for 3 to 5 days
#
1. The day before baking, roll out the dough
2. Transfer to a parchment-lined sheet pan and let them rest overnight, uncovered
#
1. Preheat the oven to 500°F, with a overturned pot and sheet pan
2. Transfer one disk of dough to a pizza peel with the dryer side (the side that was facing up) down
3. Top with sauce and cheese to the edges, add toppings
4. Transfer to the sheet pan and bake until it's crispy and browned, 10-12min
