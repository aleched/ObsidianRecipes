#Range #Vegetarian
**Batter**
- 130g (1 cup) flour
- 2½ tsp baking powder
- 1½ tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp oil
- ¾ cup water
- 250g (¼ head) tightly packed shredded green cabbage
- 6 strips bacon or other thin sliced meat

**Sauce**
- 4 Tbsp ketchup
- 3 ½ tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tbsp sugar

1. Add the dry ingredients to a large bowl, mix well
2. Add the wet ingredients to a separate bowl, mix well
3. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and mix briefly until most lumps are gone
4. Fold in the shredded cabbage
5. Heat a skillet to medium-high and lay 3 strips of bacon next to each other in it
6. Cook the bacon briefly to render some of the fat, 2min
7. Add half the batter to the bacon and spread into a ½in thick layer
8. Cook until the bottom is crisp and brown, 3-4min
9. Flip and cook the other side until crips and brown, 3-4min
10. Transfer to a plate, top with sauce, and serve immediately
11. Use the remaining bacon and batter to make a second okonomiyaki
