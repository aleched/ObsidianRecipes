#Dessert #Bar #Oven 
- 113g (8 tbsp) butter, room-temp cold
- 57g (½ cup) powdered sugar
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 120g (1 cup) flour

1. Preheat the oven to 300°
2. Lightly grease the cast iron skillet
3. Add the butter, sugar, and vanilla to the mixing bowl and beat until smooth
4. Add in the flour and beat until it comes together
5. Dough will be stiff, add up to 1 tbsp water if it won’t come together
6. Press the dough into the skillet, smoothing the surface
7. Use a fork to prick the dough all over
8. Bake 30-35min, until golden brown
9. Remove from the oven and immediately cut into wedges
10. Serve drizzled with melted caramel or chocolate, or top with jam
