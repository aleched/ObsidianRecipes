#Bread #Oven
**Dough**
- 600g (2½ cup) lukewarm water
- ½ tsp yeast
- 15g (1 tbsp) honey
- 800g (5⅓ cup) flour
- 18g (2 tbsp) kosher salt
- 50g (¼ cup) olive oil, plus more for pan and finishing
- Flaky salt for finishing  

**Brine**
- 5g (1½ tsp) Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt
- 80g (⅓ cup) lukewarm water

1. In a medium bowl, mix the water, yeast, and honey to dissolve
2. In a large bowl, mix the flour and salt
3. Add the water mixture and olive oil to the flour
4. Stir with a rubber spatula until just incorporated
5. Scrape the sides of the bowl clean and cover with plastic wrap
6. Leave out at room temperature to ferment for 12 to 14 hours until at least doubled in volume
#
1. Spread 2-3 tbsp olive oil evenly onto a sheet pan
2. Use a spatula or your hand to gently release it from the sides of the bowl and fold it onto itself gently, then pour out onto pan
3. Pour an additional 2 tbsp olive oil over the dough and gently spread across
4. Gently stretch the dough to the edge of the sheet by placing your hands underneath and pulling outward
5. Repeat stretching once or twice over the course of 30min to ensure dough remains stretched
#
1. Dimple the dough by pressing the pads of your first three fingers in at an angle
2. Make the brine and pour it over the dough to fill dimples
3. Proof for 45min until the dough is light and bubbly
#
1. 30min into this final proof, preheat the oven to 450° with the sauté pan overturned on the center rack
#
1. Sprinkle flaky salt over the dough
2. Bake for 30-35min until the bottom crust is crisp and golden brown
3. Douse with 2-3 tbsp olive oil over the whole surface
4. Let cool for 5 minutes, then release focaccia from pan with metal spatula and transfer to a cooling rack to cool completely
