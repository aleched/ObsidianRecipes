- 170g (12 tbsp) butter
- 100g (½ cup) brown sugar
- 50g (¼ cup) sugar
- 113g (⅓ cup) molasses (unsulphured or dark, not blackstrap, prefer Grandma’s brand)
- 1 egg
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 280g (2¼ cups) flour
- 1½ tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp ground ginger
- 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp ground cloves
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
- ¼ tsp salt
- granulated sugar, for rolling

1. Soften the butter and cream it with the sugars, mixing very well
2. Add the molasses, egg, and vanilla and mix well
3. Mix the dry ingredients together separately then slowly add to the wet on low speed until just combined
4. Cover the dough and chill for 1 hour or up to 3 days
#
1. Preheat oven to 350°
2. Shape dough into balls and roll in granulated sugar
3. Bake 10min or until done
