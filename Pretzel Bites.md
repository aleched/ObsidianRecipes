#Bread #Oven 
**Dough**
- 200g (2½ cup) flour
- 6g (1 tsp) salt
- 4g (1 tsp) sugar
- 7g (2¼ tsp) yeast
- 200g (⅞ cup) lukewarm water

**Topping**
- 227g (1 cup) boiling water
- 28g (2 tbsp) baking soda

1. Add all dough ingredients to the mixer and beat until well-combined
2. Use dough hook to knead until soft, smooth, and slack, 5min
3. Sprinkle with flour, cover and let rest for 30min
4. Combine the boiling water and baking soda and stir until dissolved, set aside to cool
#
1. Preheat the oven to 400°
2. Transfer the dough to a lightly greased work surface
3. Roll chunks of the dough out into ropes and cut into bite sized pieces
4. Place the bites into the solution, gently swish them around, and let sit for 3min
5. Transfer the bites to a baking sheet and sprinkle with salt or sugar
6. Bake the bites until golden brown, 12-15min
7. Remove them from the oven and roll them in ghee
8. (Optional) Toss with cinnamon-sugar
9. Store extras in a bag at room temperature
