#Beef #Range 
- 1 lb beef tenderloin
- ½ cup pear, crushed
- ¼ cup onion, pureed
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 green onion, diced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1½ tbsp rice syrup
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 carrot, sliced thinly

1. Cut the beef into ½ x 2 inch pieces that are ⅛ inch thick
2. Add all the marinade ingredients to a bowl, mix well
3. Add the sliced beef to the marinade and refrigerate 30min or overnight
4. Grill or pan fry the beef and serve with ssamjang
