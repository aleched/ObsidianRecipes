#Breakfast #Vegetarian #Range 
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 flour tortillas
- Shredded cheese
- Pickled red onions
- Shredded pork or chicken

1. Beat the eggs with the sesame oil and season with salt
2. Heat a nonstick frying pan over medium heat and coat with the vegetable oil
3. Slide one tortilla into the hot oil, then top with the eggs
4. Add additional toppings as desired, cheese, pickled red onions, meat, spinach, etc
5. Top with the other tortilla
6. Cook until the bottom tortilla is golden brown and the eggs begin to set, 1-2min
7. Flip and cook the second side until golden brown, adding more oil as necessary, 1-2min
