#Noodles #Vegetarian #Range 
- ¼ lb bacon
- ¾ lb dry spaghetti
- 3 tbsp chili garlic sauce
- 2 tbsp miso
- 2 tbsp boiling water
- 3 eggs
- 2 oz pecorino cheese
- 1 tsp black pepper

1. Boil a pot of salted water and start heating the sauté pan
2. Dice the bacon, then add to the oiled sauté pan and cook until crisp, 10min
3. Whisk the chili garlic sauce, miso, and water from the boiling pot together in a bowl
4. Add the eggs and beat until smooth
5. Cook the spaghetti until al dente and drain, reserving ½ cup of the pasta water
6. Add the pasta to the sauté pan and toss to combine
7. Add the sauce, cheese, and pepper, then reduce heat to low and toss until coated
