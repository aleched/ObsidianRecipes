#Dessert #Tofu #Pie
Filling:
- 1 block extra firm tofu
- ½-¾ cup sugar
- 1 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 250g creamy peanut butter

1. Cut tofu into chunks
2. Add everything but the peanut butter to the food processor and puree until smooth
3. Add the peanut butter and puree until smooth
4. Press the filling into the crust and refrigerate for at least 2 hours

Crust:  
- 8 tbsp (113g) butter  
- 25 Oreos
  
1. Melt butter in a bowl
2. Crush the cookies in a food processor
3. Mix the crushed cookies with the butter
4. Press the mixture into a pie pan
5. Freeze at least 30 minutes
