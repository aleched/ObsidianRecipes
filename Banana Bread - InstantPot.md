#Dessert #Bread #InstantPot 
- 200g (1½ cup) flour
- 100g (½ cup) sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 100g (½ cup) oil
- 120g (¼ cup) yogurt
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 3 bananas
- ¼ cup chocolate chips

1. Lightly spray the bundt pan with oil
2. Combine the dry ingredients, mix well
3. Add the eggs, oil, vanilla, and yogurt, mix well
4. Fold in the bananas and chocolate chips
5. Pour the batter into the bundt pan and cover with aluminum foil
6. Put the trevet inside the instant pot, add 2 cup water, then place the bundt pan on top
7. Pressure cook for 50min, natural release for 10min, then quick release
8. Cool the bread in the pan for 10min, then turn out onto a cooling rack
