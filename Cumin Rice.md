#Side #InstantPot #Vegetarian 
- 1 cup Basmati rice
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp ghee
- 1¼ cup water

1. Rinse the basmati rice 2-3 times and soak 15min
2. Use the instant pot to saute the cumin seeds in the ghee until they sizzle, 30sec
3. Drain the rice and add to the pot, saute another 30sec
4. Add the salt and water and stir
5. Pressure cook for 6min, naturally release for 10min, then quick release
