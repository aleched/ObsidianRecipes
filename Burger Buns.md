#Bread #Oven 
- 23g (3 tbsp) flour
- 113g (½ cup) water
- 395g (3⅓ cup) flour
- 163g (¾ cup) water
- 28g (2 tbsp) butter, softened
- 1 egg
- 50g (¼ cup) sugar
- 8g (1¼ tsp) salt
- 1 tbsp instant yeast

1. Add 3 tbsp flour and ½ cup water to a small saucepan
2. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until it forms a thick slurry, 1-3min
3. Add the slurry to a mixing bowl, let cool, then add the remaining ingredients
4. Beat until the dough starts to leave the sides of the bowl
5. Use dough hook to knead until smooth and passes windowpane test, 5-8min
6. Transfer to a greased bowl, cover and oven prove low 90min
#
1. Gently deflate the dough, divide into 8 pieces (~100g each), and shape into balls
2. Flatten each dough ball with the palm of your hand until ~3" across
3. Place the buns on a greased baking sheet and drape with greased plastic wrap
4. Oven prove low 45-60min, until noticeably puffy
#
1. Melt 3 tbsp of butter and brush the buns with about half
2. Bake the buns at 375° for 15-18min, until light golden brown
3. Remove the buns from the oven and brush with the remaining butter, cool on a rack
