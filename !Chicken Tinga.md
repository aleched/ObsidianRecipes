IngredientsChicken Tinga2 chicken breasts, thinned for quick cookingSaltDrizzle of oilTinga Sauce1 can (800 g) of whole tomatoes1/4 of an onion2-3 cloves of garlic2-3 chipotles in adobo & their liquidSprinkle of cuminSprinkle of oreganoSalt to tasteQuesadilla Dipping SauceSour creamPoblano hot sauceLime juiceQuesadilla ComponentsCorn tortillaCheese of choice, gratedPrepped Chicken TingaLettuce, thinly slicedTomato, dicedPickled onionsInstructionsPrep the chicken: Sprinkle each chicken piece with salt. Grill or sear in a pan until just cooked through. Shred the chicken with a fork, hands, or stand mixer until pulled into pieces. Set aside.Start the tinga sauce: Add all tinga sauce except the bay leaf ingredients to a blender, and process until smooth.Cook & combine the sauce: Set pan over medium-high heat. Add a little oil, then pour in the sauce. The sauce will sputter and start to fry — simmer for a couple of minutes or until the sauce has darkened a shade.Add the reserved shredded chicken. Lower the heat and gently simmer for at least 10-15 minutes to reduce and meld the flavors.Make the dipping sauce: Meanwhile, mix together sour cream with a hot sauce of choice and lime juice to taste. Add the hot sauce and citrus little by little, until you’ve reached your ideal flavor.Finish the chicken tinga: Once the tinga sauce has thickened and coats the chicken, turn off the heat. Taste and adjust with salt.Assemble & toast a quesadilla: Add a tortilla with/ a layer of grated cheese to a pan over medium heat. As the cheese begins to melt, fill it with a portion of chicken tinga and toppings of choice.Fold the tortilla over to enclose. Flip and continue cooking until both sides are browned, and the cheese has melted.
