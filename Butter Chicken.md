#Chicken #InstantPot #Curry 
- 1 14oz can tomatoes
- 5 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- 1 chicken breast (or 1lb paneer)
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
- 8 tbsp butter
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp garam masala

1. Add all ingredients up to the chicken or paneer to the instant pot, mix well
2. Pressure cook for 10min (+2min if frozen)
3. Naturally release for 10min, then quick release
4. Remove the chicken or paneer and set aside
5. Use immersion blender to puree the sauce
6. Add the frozen vegetables, let it cool slightly
7. Add the cut up butter, cream, and garam masala and mix well
8. Cut the chicken or paneer into small chunks and add back
