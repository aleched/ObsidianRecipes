#Range #Chicken 
- 700g chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 3 tbsp harissa
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1-1½ cup chicken stock

1. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper
2. Dice the onion
3. Heat saute pan over medium-high
4. Add the oil and chicken thighs, cook undisturbed until browned, 5-6min
5. Flip and brown the other side, 4-5min more
6. Remove the thighs and set aside
7. Add the onion and saute 2-3 minutes
8. Add the garlic and saute 30sec
9. Add the harissa and cinnamon, mix
10. Add the chicken and any juices
11. Add enough of the stock to come halfway up the chicken, and bring to a simmer
12. Partly cover and cook until the chicken reaches 160°, 15min
