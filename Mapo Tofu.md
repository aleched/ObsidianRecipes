#Range #Tofu #Pork #Beef
- ½ lb ground pork or beef
- 14 oz soft tofu or paneer
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp doenjang or gochujang
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp ginger
- 2 green onions

**Sauce**
- ¼ cup water or stock
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp cornstarch

1. Drain the tofu or paneer and cut into 1 inch cubes, and dice the green onions
2. Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small bowl
3. Heat a wok then add oil and cook the ground meat until no longer pink
4. Add the doenjang, garlic, and ginger and fry until the oil changes color
6. Add the sauce mixture and mix well
7. Bring the sauce to a gentle boil, add the tofu and gently mix until heated
8. Add the green onions and mix
