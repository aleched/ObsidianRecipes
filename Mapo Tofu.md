#Range #Tofu #Pork 
- 14 oz soft tofu
- 2 green onions
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp ginger
- ½ lb ground pork (or other meat)

**Sauce**
- ¼ cup water
- 2½ tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp miso
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- ½ tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp cornstarch

1. Drain the tofu and cut into 1 inch cubes, and dice the green onions
2. Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small bowl
3. Heat a pan to medium, then add the oil and saute the garlic and ginger until fragrant
4. Add the ground pork and break it up with a spatula
5. When the meat is cooked, add the sauce mixture and mix well
6. Bring the sauce to a gentle boil, add the tofu and gently mix until heated
7. Add the green onions and mix
